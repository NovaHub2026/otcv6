# PH-39 live evidence — a venue serving through its own phase gate

Type: EVIDENCE RECORD
Commit: `4e86136` (`main`), plus the first build of the same subphase before its
three corrections (noted per section)
Venue: the deployment on 127.0.0.1:7300, the engine the Human Owner watches,
on its real state directory
Recorded: 2026-09-25

---

## 1. Why this file exists

Cycle Audit 13's meta-auditor (a7-06) found PH-39.1 quoting measurements —
`15.9 s`, `107 s`, "12 tests, 12 passed" — that existed nowhere a reader could
re-execute or even locate, while PH-26, PH-30, PH-34, PH-35 and PH-37 all have an
evidence record. The numbers were real; the discipline was not. This is the
record they should have been written into, and `CLAUDE.md` §5 now points here.

## 2. The three occurrences that opened the phase

Three times on 2026-09-25, on ordinary developer load, the venue lost every
market and did not come back:

| UTC   | reopenings | markets stalled                                   | what ended it |
| ----- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| 04:5x | 60         | 30 of 30                                          | hand restart  |
| 11:39 | 30         | 30 of 30, `ready:false`, 110 s behind and falling | hand restart  |
| 12:06 | 120        | 30 of 30, 393 s behind                            | hand restart  |

The 11:39 and 12:06 captures are at `~/.otc-local/ph39/live-stall-2026-09-25b.txt`.
Twice the machine was already idle again while the markets stayed stalled, which
is what made it a defect rather than a slow host.

## 3. The venue through 79 minutes of its own gate

The fix was deployed **before** the phase gate, on purpose: the gate is 79
minutes of builds, 3,597 unit tests and a browser on the same host, which is the
load that had already wedged this venue three times that day. Sampled every 20 s
(`~/.otc-local/ph39/venue-under-gate.log`, 218 samples, 15:33Z–16:52Z):

|                                     |                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| load average                        | mean 3.07, max 9.97                              |
| samples reporting `degraded`        | **12 of 218**, each recovered by the next sample |
| markets stalled in the worst sample | 30 of 30                                         |
| automatic reopenings                | **600** — about one per asset every four minutes |
| re-armings in this window           | 0                                                |
| ticks published                     | 510,897                                          |
| `otc_seconds_since_last_pass`       | median 0.010 s, p90 8.1 s, **max 107.5 s**       |
| operator interventions              | **none**                                         |

A host that withholds the CPU for 107 seconds — seven times the catch-up bound —
is exactly the condition that ended three times that day with thirty dead markets
and a person restarting the service.

**The re-arming path itself** was exercised earlier the same afternoon, on the
first build of the subphase under the unit suite: 239 reopenings, **1 re-arming**,
thirty markets serving throughout. By 19:40Z, on the merged build, the counters
read **279 reopenings and 21 re-armings** with `otc_markets_stalled 0` — 21
passes that under the old code would each have ended the venue's life.

## 4. What the gate cost, and the finding in it

The first gate on this tree went **red** on three statistical tests — two browser
assertions reading an empty chart, one stream resume refused — while this venue
was publishing thirty markets on the same box. The rpc probe recorded a **15.9 s**
block inside the browser worker; the gauge above recorded the host withholding
the CPU for up to 107 s. The same two files alone on a quiet host:

```
LD_LIBRARY_PATH=… OTC_REQUIRE_BROWSER=1 npx vitest run --project statistical \
  --no-file-parallelism apps/api/src/servedRecord.stat.test.ts apps/web/src/panel.stat.test.ts
  ->  Test Files 2 passed (2)   Tests 12 passed (12)
```

Then the full gate with the engine stopped: `GATE_EXIT=0`, unit 175 files / 3,597
tests / 156 s, coverage the same with the floors enforced, statistical 47 files /
411 tests / 3,928 s. **Every gate measured on this machine before 2026-09-25 ran
beside a venue that was wedged, and a wedged venue consumes nothing.**

## 5. What this evidence does not say

- **It is one host.** A deployment that does not share a box with a build farm
  would see far fewer outages, and one that shares a worse box would see more.
- **The seams are the cost, and they are large here.** Cycle Audit 13 (a8-05)
  measured what 600 reopenings mean for a broker: on `eurusd-otc`, 57 seams in 24
  hours, **268.7 minutes — 18.66% of the day — inside a recorded discontinuity**
  where `settle()` refuses and `/price` answers 409, against contracts of 30 s to
  15 m. That is ADR-0020's trade, not this subphase's invention, but the number
  belongs beside the recovery it bought.
- **The boot writes two seams per asset** on this venue, not one
  ([#23](https://github.com/NovaHub2026/otcv6/issues/23)): 30 boot seams at
  12:31:40Z and 30 automatic reopenings at 12:32:06Z, because `start()` itself
  takes longer than the 15 s bound.
