# PH-14.2 — Followers Serve the Record

Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL DOCUMENT
Identifier: PH-14.2
Parent: [PH-14](PH-14-multi-node.md)
Status: APPROVED
Created: 2026-09-01
Decisions applied: [ADR-0012](../decisions/ADR-0012-single-writer-generation.md)

## 1. Objective

Let a node that does not lead an asset still answer questions about it, and make
its answers identical to the leader's for every instant it covers.

## 2. A follower must be unable to generate, not merely unwilling

[PH-14](PH-14-multi-node.md) §9 names the hazard: "a follower silently
generating would fork invisibly". A comment saying it does not is worth nothing
against a future edit that adds an engine "just for the gap".

So the follower is a module that **cannot import one**. `follower.ts` depends on
`@otc/core` for the tick type and on nothing else — no `@otc/engine`, no
`MasterKeyring`, no configuration that could produce a price. A guardrail
asserts the import list, so adding an engine to the follower fails the suite
rather than passing review.

That also settles INV-010 for followers by construction: a module that never
receives key material cannot leak it.

## 3. Replication is the record, not a side channel

Followers replicate through the same store that carries the lease. The leader
appends its published ticks; followers read them. There is no second channel and
no gossip, because a second path to the same data is a second opportunity for
the two to disagree.

The append is **fenced**. A stranded leader that has lost its lease cannot add to
the record, which is the same guarantee PH-14.1 gave the checkpoint, applied to
the thing observers actually see.

## 4. Re-appending is not the same as forking, and the difference is the point

A leader that crashes after appending but before checkpointing regenerates ticks
when it resumes — deliberately, because deterministic replay reproduces them
identically, which is what makes the resume path safe (PH-5.2).

So the log will be asked to append sequences it already holds. Two cases, and
conflating them would be the worst possible error:

- **Identical ticks** — accept, silently and idempotently. This is replay
  working as designed.
- **A different tick at a sequence already recorded** — refuse, loudly, with
  `RecordForkError`. This is INV-003 broken: two different underlying streams
  claiming the same asset and the same sequence. It is the exact signature two
  leaders would leave, and it must never be repaired by overwriting.

Overwriting would make a fork look like a successful write. Refusing makes it an
incident.

## 5. "Behind" and "impossible" are different answers

`UnknownSequenceError` (PH-7, Cycle Audit 3) tells a client asking for a
sequence past the newest that it "is not behind — it is holding a record this
feed did not produce". On a single node that is exactly right.

Across nodes it becomes a false accusation. A client that read sequence 8,000
from the leader and then reconnected to a follower still at 7,950 is behind
_nothing of its own doing_: the follower is. Telling it that it holds a
fabricated record would send a correct client to reset a correct history.

A follower therefore answers with three cases rather than two, distinguished by
the record head it can read from the shared store:

| Request                                | Answer                              |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Within what the follower holds         | the ticks                           |
| Beyond the follower, within the record | `lagging`, with both heads reported |
| Beyond the record itself               | `unknown` — the phantom case        |

The middle row is new and is the whole reason this section exists.

## 6. INV-002 across nodes

The claim to prove is the product's most visible promise: same asset, same
moment, same price, whichever node you reach.

It decomposes into two properties, and both are tested against a real engine
rather than a stub:

- **Prefix.** A follower's history is always a prefix of the leader's. Never a
  different tick at the same sequence, never a gap, never ahead.
- **Agreement.** For every instant the follower covers, the price it reports
  equals the leader's — which follows from prefix, and is asserted directly
  anyway, because "follows from" is how PH-7's proof came to rest on a premise
  that a restart falsifies.

## 7. What is built

`packages/runtime/src/replication.ts`

- `ReplicationLog` — `appendTicks` (fenced), `readTicks`, `recordHead`.
- `RecordForkError`.

`packages/runtime/src/follower.ts`

- `FollowerMarket` — `pull`, `serve`, `priceAt`, `head`. No engine, no key.
- `ServeResult` — `ticks` | `lagging` | `unknown`.

`MemoryCoordinatedStore` gains the log, keeping lease and record in one store
for the reason PH-14.1 §5 gives.

## 8. Verification

| Claim                                                           | How                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| The follower module imports no engine and no key material       | guardrail over the import list      |
| A leader that lost its lease cannot append                      | replication battery                 |
| Re-appending identical ticks is accepted idempotently           | replication battery                 |
| A differing tick at a recorded sequence is refused              | replication battery                 |
| A gap in an append is refused                                   | replication battery                 |
| A follower's history is a prefix of the leader's at every step  | INV-002 test, real engine, 5 assets |

| A client ahead of the record is told `unknown`                  | follower battery                    |

## 9. Out of scope

Failover, the seam it creates, and the loop that renews leases and appends on a
schedule. Those are PH-14.3.

## 10. Approval

**APPROVED** 2026-09-01, from executed evidence.

| Check                          | Command                         | Exit |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ---- |
| Formatting                     | `npm run format:check`          | 0    |
| Build and typecheck            | `npm run build`                 | 0    |
| Lint (type-aware, after build) | `npm run lint`                  | 0    |
| Unit suite, whole workspace    | `npx vitest run --project unit` | 0    |

64 test files, 1,276 tests. This subphase adds 55 unit tests plus 4 guardrail
tests, and the INV-002 test drives the real engine across all five catalogue
assets for ten minutes of market time.

### Each guard watched failing

Twelve defects planted in turn, each reverted before the next.

| Planted defect                                                       | Tests that failed |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Append accepts a differing tick at a recorded sequence (a fork)      | 3                 |
| Append closes a gap instead of refusing it                           | 4                 |
| Append is unfenced                                                   | 4                 |
| Append mutates the record before validating the whole batch          | 1                 |
| Follower closes a replication gap                                    | 2                 |
| Follower reports `lagging` as `unknown`                              | 3                 |
| Follower reports `evicted` as `lagging`                              | 1                 |
| Follower extrapolates a price backwards before its first tick        | 2                 |
| `sameTick` ignores the price                                         | 3                 |
| Follower imports the engine directly                                 | 2 guardrail       |
| Follower reaches the engine through one level of indirection         | 2 guardrail       |
| Follower takes a keyring as a parameter without importing the engine | 1 guardrail       |

The last three matter most. The first draft of the single-writer guardrail
checked `follower.ts` alone and would have passed the indirection plant; it walks
the transitive closure because of that, and the identifier check exists because
the import check alone misses a keyring arriving as a parameter.

### Found by the new guardrail during the subphase

The single-writer guard failed on its first run, on real code: the path
`follower.ts -> replication.ts -> lease.ts -> state.ts -> @otc/engine` existed
through type-only imports. Type-only is precisely why it would have been waved
through, and it is one word from being real.

Fixed by extracting `fence.ts`. That is a better factoring on its own terms —
PH-14.1 §2 already argued that leasing and fencing are different mechanisms —
but it was the guard that found it, not the argument.

### Found by the lease during the subphase

The INV-002 test failed on its first run with `StaleFenceError`: it advanced the
clock through ten minutes of market time without renewing, so the lease expired
after fifteen seconds and the fence refused the append. The mechanism working
against its own author. `tickClock` renews, and says why in a comment.

### Fixed in scope

The first INV-002 test compared each follower's whole history against the
leader's at every step, which is quadratic and timed out at 120 steps. Replaced
with an incremental check from a verified cursor — every tick is compared once,
at the moment it is applied, which is where a divergence appears — plus one deep
comparison of the full history at the end, which proves nothing was rewritten
behind the cursor.
