## 1. Objective

Establish that the re-authored catalogue still satisfies every guarantee the
project has made, and bring the living documentation into line with what is now
true.

## 2. Why this is a subphase and not a gate step

PH-10.2 changed the market process. Not its structure — no layer was added and
no input was newly observed — but its parameters, for all five assets at once,
including the lattice each one settles on.

Every claim the project holds was measured on the old process:

| Claim                      | Where it was established        |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| INV-006, per asset         | PH-4.3, against the battery     |
| INV-006, withheld families | PH-9.1                          |
| Realism band, per asset    | PH-4.3                          |
| INV-002 across observers   | PH-7.2                          |
| INV-009 settlement         | PH-6.1, on the published record |
| Restart seam continuity    | PH-5.2, PH-9.1                  |

None of those transfer for free. Budgeting them as a subphase rather than as
"the gate will catch it" is the difference between revalidation and hope.

## 3. The one that must not be quietly re-run

PH-9 §5 forbids tuning against the withheld families. This subphase **runs** them
on the new catalogue, which is allowed and necessary — but if one of them
reports, the correct response is to record a finding, not to adjust a rhythm
until it stops reporting. Doing that once would convert the withheld families
into what the main battery already is: a test that has been used as a target, and
therefore no longer independent evidence.

The rhythms in PH-10.2 were chosen against none of this. They were chosen for
what the ladders mean, and the analytic gate accepted them before anything was
simulated.

## 4. Scope

- The full quality gate: format, lint, build, both test projects.
- The withheld red-team families, on the re-authored catalogue.
- Living documentation: `INVARIANTS.md`'s INV-007 row and its narrative,
  `MARKET_MODEL.md`'s account of the cascade, `CURRENT_STATE.md`'s limitations.
- `docs/BACKLOG.md`: B-004 closed with the measurement that closed it.
- Phase approval record for PH-10.

## 5. Exclusions

- Any further engine change. If revalidation finds something, that is a finding
  and a decision, not a silent retune.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. `npm run gate` exits 0 on the re-authored catalogue.
2. The withheld families return a clean verdict, and the verdict is recorded with
   its hypothesis count and worst statistic.
3. Every living document that quoted a superseded figure is corrected; every
   historical phase record is left intact per `GOVERNANCE.md` §47.
4. B-004 is closed with evidence, or explicitly not closed with a reason.

### Two defects the gate found that nothing else would have

**Four consistency tests failed for a reason unrelated to consistency.**
`distributionConsistency.stat.test.ts` asserted hardcoded tick counts — `> 2000`,
`> 1000` — that silently encoded asset 0's realised pace. Re-authoring its rhythm
moved that pace and they broke. The thresholds are now derived from the
catalogue's own recorded `meanIntervalMs`, so they cannot rot the same way again.

Investigating why produced something worth recording: realised pace under a short
hosted run is about 30% slower than the calibrated stationary mean, far more than
the 6.5% the calibration figure itself moved. A hosted market starts with **zero
Hawkes excitation** and needs several excitation memories to reach its stationary
rate — and `arrivalMemoryMs` is a per-asset trait now, so eurusd's doubling from
120 s to 240 s lengthened that transient. These runs measure the transient. That
is fine for testing agreement and wrong to encode as a literal.

**B-005 came back, and I wrote it.** The gate reported
`Test Files 71 passed, Tests 1108 passed` and exited 1, with
`Error: [vitest-worker]: Timeout calling "onTaskUpdate"` above the green summary.
`latticeTies.stat.test.ts` drove btcusd for 25 seconds inside a synchronous test
body and starved the worker's own RPC channel. Fixed by the documented
convention: async callbacks yielding every 250,000 ticks.

**No guardrail was added for it, deliberately.** The condition is not statically
visible: the offending loop is bounded at 8,000 iterations while
`distributionConsistency.stat.test.ts` loops 800 times and is fine — the
difference is unbounded per-iteration cost. Any static rule would miss this case
or flag the healthy ones, and a guard that cannot catch its own defect is the
most repeated finding in this project's history. Writing one here would have
produced a seventh instance rather than preventing one.

The gate detects this correctly; the cost is diagnosis time. So the convention
and the exact error string went into `CLAUDE.md` §5, where an agent reads them
before writing tests, and **B-010** records that B-005 fixed a cause while the
class is standing.
