## 1. Objective

_"Viendo las velas que está generando el gráfico hay muchos gaps: el precio de
cierre y apertura de las velas están muy distantes la mayoría de las veces."_
Measured on the local engine (six hours of 1m history, one minute of live
ticks) before this subphase:

| Asset (1m) | ticks per candle, median | gap / range, median | candles with gap > 25 % of range |
| ---------- | -----------------------: | ------------------: | -------------------------------: |
| EUR/USD    |                       36 |                0.08 |                             16 % |
| GBP/JPY    |                       52 |               ~0.05 |                             ~8 % |
| BTC/USD    |                      106 |                0.04 |                              3 % |
| S&P 500    |                       17 |                ~0.1 |                            ~20 % |
| Gold       |                       23 |                0.12 |                             26 % |

The gap is one tick step — a candle opens on the first tick inside it and the
previous one closed on the last tick before it (`packages/chart/src/bars.ts`)
— and it is visible because the engine's markets have **few, large ticks**:
EUR/USD's live median step was ~0.2 pip with a 90th percentile near 0.6 pip,
at 36 ticks a minute; a real EUR/USD quote stream moves ~0.1 pip a tick at a
few hundred ticks a minute. The relation across assets is the diagnosis: the
more ticks a candle holds, the smaller its boundary gap relative to its range.
Regimes scale the step and so the gap, but the median gap is there in every
regime. This is not the chart and not the regime; it is tick granularity.

The Human Owner chose to fix the engine inside the Lab phase — _"el Lab todavía
necesita trabajo pero es importante que el motor funcione bien; vamos con el
motor en esta misma fase"_ (DECISION-LOG, 2026-09-03).

## 2. What is built

- **A measure first.** `packages/lab` gains a tick-granularity metric — ticks
  per minute bar, boundary gap over candle range (median and the share above a
  quarter), the step's median and 90th percentile — reported by the Lab's
  quality route and shown on the Calidad tab, so the before and the after are
  the same measurement.
- **The calibration, decided from the measurement.** Each catalogue asset was
  re-authored at a fraction of its tempo with the tick RMS divided by the square
  root of the same factor (`authorPersonality`, the registration keyring, so
  the recorded traits stay a fixed point of the solve); the structure traits —
  cascade depth, span, spacing, regimes — are untouched, and the recorded
  lattice quantum and display precision are kept, so a running market's record
  stays on its lattice. Factors: **÷4** for EUR/USD, S&P 500 and Gold; **÷3**
  for GBP/JPY (÷4 put its aggregational-gaussianity ratio at 1.03, out of band,
  over 24 M ticks; ÷3 sits at 0.69) and for BTC/USD (÷4 crosses the 250 ms
  trait floor). Family tempo ranges in `families.ts` divided the same way for
  future registrations; the brief derives the tick RMS from the family's
  dispersion and the tempo, so the quarterly dispersion is what stays fixed.

  Six hours simulated per asset, before → after (medians unless said):

  | Asset   | ticks / 1m candle | gap / range | candles with gap > ¼ of range | step p90 (lattice, re-measured) |
  | ------- | ----------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
  | EUR/USD | 39 → 166          | 0.08 → 0.04 | 12 % → 1 %                    | 72 → 33                         |
  | GBP/JPY | 61 → 168          | 0.06 → 0.04 | 8 % → 1 %                     | 111 → 61 (÷3)                   |
  | BTC/USD | 149 → 366         | 0.04 → 0.02 | 2 % → 0 %                     | 58 → 38 (÷3)                    |

  > `44 → 97` for GBP/JPY and `42 → 54` for BTC/USD: the two ÷3 assets recorded
  > as if the recalibration had made their ticks _larger_, which is the reverse
  > of what dividing the tick RMS by √3 on the same lattice does, and the
  > reverse of this subphase's own headline. The pair was not transposed back
  > but re-measured, because the original sampling could not support either
  > direction: eight seeds per cell, 48 hours of each market's life per seed,
  > the median across seeds, "before" reconstructed by inverting the
  > recalibration on the shipped configuration — tempo × f, tick RMS × √f, the
  > recorded quantum kept. Every asset's step falls, by 0.46, 0.55, 0.66,
  > 0.49 and 0.54 in catalogue order, against the 1/2 and 1/√3 ≈ 0.58 the
  > arithmetic predicts. **Six hours was too short a window to read a p90
  > from** — one regime dominates it, and the per-seed values for EUR/USD alone
  > span 44–122 before and 25–61 after — which is how a pair the wrong way
  > round could sit in an approved record and contradict the paragraph above it
  > unnoticed. The other four columns are the subphase's own six-hour sampling,
  > re-measured on the published catalogue in §4.

The dispersion ratios sit inside `catalogue.stat`'s 0.7–1.4 band and the
fresh quanta inside its 0.6–1.6 band; the tie rate at 30 s on the recorded
lattice roughly halves (finer ticks land exactly on the open less often), and
`MEASURED_LATTICE_TIE_RATES` is re-measured with the test's own procedure.

The fifteen realism metrics over **24 million ticks** per asset, re-authored:
EUR/USD 15/15 (aggregational-gaussianity 0.58), GBP/JPY ÷3 15/15 (0.69),
BTC/USD ÷3 15/15 (0.67), S&P 500 15/15 (0.55), Gold 15/15 (0.71).

**A finding for the audit, not a change.** `aggregational-gaussianity`
aggregates sixty _ticks_; the stylized fact is about _time_. Under a faster
tempo the sixty-tick window shrinks to seconds and the ratio rises even as
the sixty-**second** ratio falls (GBP/JPY at ÷4: sixty-tick 0.81 → 0.70 on
3 M ticks, sixty-second 2.36 → 1.23; EUR/USD 1.63 → 1.22). The metric is
tempo-dependent. It is left as it is here — this subphase loosens nothing —
and recorded for Cycle Audit 8.

- **What denser ticks broke, and how each is answered.** The full gate on
  the recalibrated catalogue failed four statistical tests, every one a sample
  or a window sized in _ticks_ that the finer grain had shrunk in time:
  - the Lab's default quality sample (a million ticks: 129 hypotheses against 378) is now a **span** — the sixteen days a million ticks were on EUR/USD —
    in the asset's own ticks, bounded at eight million;
  - the assurance record (`tools/sim/assurance.stat.test.ts`) spans the same
    six and a half days it did, not 400,000 ticks;
  - the sampled catalogue's signature windows are 46 minutes of each asset,
    not 2,000 ticks; its whole-catalogue separation had fallen to 0.1240
    against the 0.125 it must beat;
  - and the exact close: at 20 ticks a second the parity of a window flips
    within a second of a preview, so the board flow's neighbour — reachable when
    offered — was off-parity when applied. An **apply now takes the reachable
    neighbour on the requested side** (one lattice step) and names the
    adjustment (`adjusted`, on the screen as «ajustado por paridad»); a preview
    still names both neighbours. "Minimum" is not redefined: the request is
    answered with the nearest reachable level, said out loud.

- **Continuity.** Whatever a hosted market's traits are, they are what its
  state record carries; the change reaches a market through the seam the
  runtime already has for a configuration that no longer matches its record,
  documented here once read.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch the sign path or anything the mirror test guards (ADR-0003).
- Loosen a statistical assertion to make a new range pass.
- Change the chart's convention to hide the gap: the record is what it is.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The Lab reports tick granularity per market, before and after.
2. After the calibration, on the shipped catalogue: median ticks per 1m candle
   at least three times the current for every family; boundary gap / range
   median ≤ 0.03 and candles with gap > 25 % of range ≤ 5 % on every asset;
   quarterly dispersion per family within its calibrated range.
3. Mirror test unchanged and green; the full statistical suite green.
4. Guards, plants, **full** gate (`npm run gate`), not a targeted one.

> hueco/rango ≤ 0,07 y ≤ 8% de velas por encima de un cuarto. Devolver el tempo
> a su valor anterior lo hace fallar con exactamente los números de la queja
> original.
