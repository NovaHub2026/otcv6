## 1. Objective

The Human Owner stopped the Lab on 2026-09-03 to say what it is for, in two
sentences: _push or pull the current price by chosen amounts, with buttons, as
often as I like, the market otherwise walking its own path — and make the
current candle close at a price I choose_. And one condition on the first:
_"los empujes o retrocesos deben ser naturales … los movimientos que él hace
después deben ser naturales"_.

The second exists (PH-24.2, PH-24.7). The first does not, and the condition
decides its design. A push is **not** a jump: nothing is added to a price. A
push of `+N` is the market's next `N` ticks taking the upward sign — their own
magnitudes, their own intervals, drawn by the engine exactly as they would have
been — and then the keystream again. It is the fastest movement that is still
the engine's, because every step of it is one of the engine's steps; it is
repeatable (a second `+N` while the first runs extends it); and it is honest
about what it costs, since a run of `N` equal signs is what a chart will show.
The mechanism is the one every Lab control already uses, `SelectableSigns`
playing a vector (PH-24.1); this subphase adds the vector `[+1 × N]`.

The screen is reorganised around the two controls. Everything else the Lab
grew — board, positions, scenarios, quality, session — stays, one tab further
back.

## 2. What is built

- **`POST /lab/markets/:id/push?ticks=<±N>`**, `1 ≤ |N| ≤ 50`. Arms `N` signs
  of the given direction; a push in the same direction while one runs
  **extends** it (`SelectableSigns.extend`), a push in the opposite direction
  replaces what remains. Refused with `CLOSE_ARMED` while an exact close, a
  preset or a scenario is armed — two scripts cannot both describe the next
  tick — and an exact close, preset or scenario is refused with `PUSH_RUNNING`
  while a push runs, because their plans are computed on a fork that draws the
  keystream where the market would play the push. `release` ends a push like
  anything else armed. Returns the direction, the ticks armed, what remains,
  and **the landing**: the price the market will show when the push ends,
  computed on a fork playing the same signs — the magnitudes are the engine's
  and the Lab may look.
- **`control` and `control` (all)** carry `pushing: { direction, remaining }
| null`; the asset badge shows a running push.
- **The Empujar strip** under the header, visible on every tab: `−10 −5 −3 −1 |
+1 +3 +5 +10`, the running state, the landing after a click, a refusal named
  when a close is armed. The **Cierre** tab is first and default.
- Every push is a session action (`push`) with §78's fields.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Add anything to a price, insert a tick, or touch a magnitude or an interval
  (§37 stays refused; ADR-0003; DECISION-LOG 2026-09-03).
- Exist outside the Lab composition (ADR-0015 §3; `composition.test.ts`).
- Report a landing that was not computed on the engine's own fork.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. A `+N` push produces exactly `N` upward ticks from the next draw, then the
   keystream; `−N` the reverse; magnitudes and intervals are those of the same
   market without the push (the mirror argument, asserted on a fork).
2. A second push in the same direction extends; the opposite replaces;
   `release` ends it. The landing equals the price after the `N`th pushed tick.
3. Push while a close is armed → `CLOSE_ARMED`; close while pushing →
   `PUSH_RUNNING`; both recorded as refused actions.
4. From the screen: `+3` reads as running, then the header shows the landing
   price; the refusal is visible when a close is armed. Cierre is the default
   tab.
5. Guards, plants, targeted gate.
