## 1. Objective

Minutes after PH-24.10 was approved the Human Owner reported, testing the
strip: _"hubo un momento que se desactivaron y no me deja clicar hasta
ahora"_. The Lab had nothing armed — all five markets on their keystream — so
the buttons were held by the panel's `busy` state, which one handler sets
before a request and clears after it. The request that never came back was one
of many rapid pushes sent while this session restarted the panel and the Lab
process to serve the new build; the panel's API layer does not catch a failed
`fetch` or a non-JSON body, the exception escaped the handler, and `busy` was
never cleared. A reload frees it. A screen that can be locked by one failed
request is a defect, whoever caused the failure.

Two more things follow from the same session. The buttons were also disabled
whenever a close, preset or scenario was armed, with no word on the strip about
why — a refusal the operator could not even trigger to read. And the Human
Owner asked for «Objetivo de precio» to leave the Cierre tab so that Cierre is
only the close of a candle.

## 2. What is built

- **The API layer never throws.** `labGet` and `labPost` return the
  `Unavailable` shape on a failed `fetch` or an unparsable body, with the reason.
  Every handler clears `busy` in a `finally`.
- **The strip is held only by its own act.** Push buttons are disabled while a
  push request is in flight — not by a quality run, not by an armed close.
- **A push wins over what is armed.** A push on a market with a close, preset
  or scenario armed **releases it** — recorded as a `release` action with
  `by: 'push'`, the discarded count named — and arms the push; the response and
  the strip say what was released. The close's pending outcome is dropped: a
  close the operator superseded is not a close that failed. `CLOSE_ARMED` is
  gone. The reverse stays: a close, preset or scenario while a push runs is
  refused with `PUSH_RUNNING`, because a push is seconds long and its plan
  would be wrong.
- **«Objetivo de precio»** moves from the Cierre tab to Escenarios, unchanged.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Change what a push is (PH-24.10 §2) or touch a magnitude or an interval.
- Release anything without recording it.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. A failed request leaves every button usable and the reason on screen.
2. A push on a market with a close armed releases the close (recorded, counted)
   and arms the push; `lastApplied` no longer judges the superseded close.
3. Cierre contains only the close; Objetivo de precio is on Escenarios and its
   browser flow passes from there.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate.

**The incident, closed.** The Lab had nothing armed when the buttons were held
(live `control`: five markets on their keystream); the requests that never came
back were pushes sent while this session restarted the panel and the Lab to
serve the new build; `labGet`/`labPost` threw on the failed fetch and the
handler never cleared `busy`. Neither can happen now: the API layer answers
`Unavailable` with the reason, the handler clears `busy` in `finally`, and the
strip is held only by its own request.
