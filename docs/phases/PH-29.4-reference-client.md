# PH-29.4 — The Reference Client And Settlement Library: Exercised By The Release, #11 Decided

## 1. Objective

Give a broker's engineer the client they would otherwise write: one class over
`fetch` that subscribes with an exact resume, reads history, asks the two
settlement prices and verifies a proof — and a settlement library whose money
is an integer in a named unit (Issue #11), so that the reference settlement a
broker embeds cannot round differently from theirs.

## 2. What is built

**`VenueClient`** in `@otc/client`: `health()`, `markets()`, `catalogue()`,
`history(id, timeframe, from, to)`, `tick(id, sequence)`, `priceAt(id, at)`,
`proof(id, sequence)` returning a verified proof or a named refusal, and
`subscribe(id, { from, onGap })` — an async iterator of ticks that reconnects
from the last delivered sequence plus one and surfaces a told gap as an event
rather than a hole. Every method checks the response against the contract's
shape and throws a `ContractViolation` naming the route and the departure.

**Money in `packages/trading` (Issue #11).** `Contract.stake` becomes an
integer in the broker's minor unit (`stakeMinor`), the payout a rational
(`payoutNumerator`/`payoutDenominator`), and the settlement's `returned` and
`net` integers computed exactly with a documented rounding (the payout is
rounded half-up to the minor unit); `tally` sums integers. The decision is
recorded in the decision log before the code changes.

**The release exercises the client**: `conformance.stat.test.ts` reads the
venue through `VenueClient`, and the reference settlement of a contract opened
at a price the client asked for and expired at a price the client asked for
equals `settle()` over the ticks the client subscribed to.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. `VenueClient.subscribe` across a served gap yields the gap as an event and
   continues from `resumesAt`; across a dropped connection it resumes from the
   last delivered sequence plus one with no duplicate and no hole.
2. Settlement in minor units: a hundred thousand cent stakes agree with exact
   rational arithmetic; the floating-point disagreement the audit measured (124
   of 100 000) is zero.
3. The client refuses a response that departs from the contract, by name.
4. Every guard watched failing.
