## 1. Objective

Close PH-34: verify the phase as a whole, say in the broker's own guide what
changes under a running deployment, and release it.

## 2. What is done here

- **The integrated phase verification**: the full gate on the phase tip, with
  the battery and the mirror test inside it, and hosted CI on the merge — the
  two layers `GOVERNANCE.md` §34 asks for.
- **The integration guide** (`docs/integration/INTEGRATION.md`, the broker's,
  in Spanish): the tick rates an integrator should expect per asset and per
  regime, the refund rate that halved, and what the upgrade does to a running
  market — one seam per asset, no price jump, because every lattice is kept
  and the engine model is part of the checkpoint fingerprint.
- **The release**: `v2.2.0` tagged after hosted CI is green on the merge, with
  its record beside the others in `docs/evidence/`.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

Change the engine. Anything the verification finds is a finding about PH-34.1,
recorded and fixed there, not smoothed over here.
