# PH-24.7 — Los controles que faltan en pantalla

## 1. Objective

From the Lab inventory of 2026-09-03 (the list the Human Owner asked for, to
map what is missing), the items that are **controls an operator would reach for
and cannot yet reach from the screen** — each already possible by API or by
construction, none yet a button:

1. **Shock** (§F): locate the next step of at least N lattice steps in a window
   and choose its direction — `name=shock` on the scenario route, absent from
   the Escenarios tab.
2. **Close at an arbitrary expiry** (§I7): `expiry=` on the close routes,
   reachable from the screen only through a position's preset.
3. **Target Price** (§G): reach a level without a fixed time — `touches(level)`
   exists as a criterion inside breakouts; the specification names it as its
   own control, distinct from Candle Close Control (G8), and the audit found
   that distinction correctly kept. It gets its own button, and stays a
   _region reach_: strength is the acceptance rate, never a mode.

## 2. What each is, in the Lab's terms

- **Shock**: a row on Escenarios with `tamaño ≥ (pasos)` and `dirección`; a
  preview that says _cuándo_ (the tick index and the instant) or _no viene
  ninguno en esta ventana_; apply arms the direction. The sentence that it
  cannot be ordered stays behind ⓘ.
- **Expiry**: a third addressing mode on Cierre beside _vela actual_ and _vela
  siguiente_ — _a una hora_, with a time field (HH:MM:SS UTC, today) resolved
  to an instant on the client and sent as `expiry=`; the plan shows the instant
  it resolved to.
- **Target Price**: a section on Cierre — _tocar un nivel_ — with a price or a
  `delta`, a window, preview (acceptance rate, ticks in window, the selected
  path's high/low) and apply through the scenario route with the `touches`
  criterion. No terminal condition, ever: the plan says so.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Turn Target Price into a close with a hidden deadline (G8).
- Offer a shock as anything but _locate and direct_.
- Add English to the surface (`panelUx.test.ts`).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. Shock previewed and applied from the screen; "none coming" shown as a state.
2. A close at a typed time applied from the screen and landing there exactly.
3. Target Price previewed and applied from the screen; the plan states no
   terminal condition; the acceptance rate shown.
4. Both browser suites extended by one flow each for 1–3; the guards extended.
5. Targeted gate.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/api/src/lab/scenarios.ts` — `target-price` as a catalogue entry over
  `touches`, with a `level` parameter in lattice steps or resolved from a price
- `apps/web/src/app/lab/{Cierre,Escenarios}.tsx`, `Lab.tsx`, `lib/es.ts`
- `apps/web/src/labScreen.test.ts`, `lab.stat.test.ts`

**Excluded** (later subphases): session persistence and export (§78), §70 over
positions, the boundary-tick indicator (ADR-0017), several assets armed at
once, replay and snapshot (§74–§77), large runs as jobs (§67).

## 6. Result and approval

**APPROVED** 2026-09-03, from executed runs:

- **Target Price** (`scenarios.ts`, `lab.controller.ts`): a seventeenth catalogue
  entry over `touches`, addressed by steps (`level`) or by price (resolved
  against the lattice like a close, refused with its two neighbours when it is
  not a level, and kept as an absolute level so the plan measures the distance
  from where the market stands when read). `scenarioRoutes.test.ts` (6 tests):
  applied by steps, the published ticks inside the window reached the level and
  the path's end was the engine's; previewed by price, the level resolved.
- **The screen** (`labScreen.test.ts`, 11 tests): a third addressing mode on
  Cierre — _a una hora_, a typed UTC time resolved on the client to `expiry=`;
  a Target Price section visibly apart from the close, never routed through the
  close route, stating it has no terminal condition; a shock row on Escenarios
  with size, direction and _where it comes_ or _none is coming_. Plants watched
  failing: Target Price sent to `/close?` (a close with a hidden deadline); the
  no-terminal-condition line removed; the shock never saying none is coming.
- **In Chromium** (`lab.stat.test.ts`, now 6 flows, 99 s): a shock of 100,000
  steps reported as _No viene_; one of size 1 located (_viene en el tick N_) and
  its direction chosen; a close at a typed time forty seconds out armed and
  landing `EXACTO`; Target Price previewed with _sin condición de cierre_ and
  applied. `panel.stat.test.ts` 8 of 8 beside it.
- **A defect the failing flow's screen text showed**: the Target Price plan
  printed the lattice index under _nivel a tocar_ — PH-23.5 §6's defect in a new
  row. The route now returns `targetPrice` beside `targetLevel`, the screen
  prints the price and keeps the index behind ⓘ, and the guard forbids the
  index under that label.
- **Targeted gate**: `format:check → build → typecheck:web → typecheck:config →
lint → test:unit`, exit **0**, 114 files, 2,456 tests, then both browser suites
  on the same still tree.
