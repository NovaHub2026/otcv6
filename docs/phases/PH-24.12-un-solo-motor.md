# PH-24.12 — Un solo motor: el Lab es el motor en modo simulación

Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL DOCUMENT
Identifier: PH-24.12
Parent: [PH-24](PH-24-the-labs-controls.md)
Status: ACTIVE
Created: 2026-09-03

---

## 1. Objective

The Human Owner, testing the strip against Vista: _"el activo de lab tiene que
ser el mismo del motor … si yo escojo ese en lab todo lo que pase con él se
tiene que reflejar en el preview también; ahora hay dos precios diferentes como
si los activos no estuvieran conectados"_. They were right about the symptom
and its cause is the local deployment, not the Lab: `~/.otc-local/start.sh`
runs **two** processes — a production-composed engine on 7300, which Vista
shows, and a Lab-composed engine on 7302 with its own state directory, which the
Lab controls. Two engines, two EUR/USDs.

The architecture already says what the fix is (ADR-0015 §3): the Lab is not a
second engine, it is **the engine composed with a selectable sign source**. A
Lab-composed process serves every engine route — catalogue, history, the tick
stream, health — and `/lab/*` besides. The browser suite has run exactly that
shape since PH-23.5: one Lab-composed process, the panel's `OTC_API_BASE` and
`OTC_LAB_BASE` both pointing at it. The local deployment did not, out of a
caution that made sense when the Lab was new and now costs the Human Owner the
thing the Lab is for. ADR-0018 records the rule: **one engine per deployment;
a Lab-composed engine is the whole engine in simulation mode, declared as such
everywhere; production is never Lab-composed**, and that last part is the
architecture test that already exists.

## 2. What is built

- **`lab.main.ts` listens on `OTC_LAB_PORT ?? PORT`** and says in its banner
  that it serves the engine's routes too. The local launcher starts **one**
  process, `lab.main.js`, on the engine's port and the engine's state
  directory, and points the panel at it for both bases. `OTC_LAB=0` starts the
  production composition instead, and the panel then says no Lab is configured.
- **The panel declares Lab mode.** When `OTC_LAB_BASE` is the same origin as
  `OTC_API_BASE`, every screen carries the `OTC LAB — SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT`
  banner: Vista is showing the Lab-composed engine, and what the Lab does is
  what Vista shows. This is the deployment's own declaration, read server-side,
  not a guess.
- **The chart inside the Lab.** The Lab screen shows the selected market's
  chart — the same `PreviewChart` Vista uses — read from the Lab's own engine
  routes through a panel proxy to `OTC_LAB_BASE` (`/labengine/*`, GET only). So
  a push or a close is seen on the candles where it happens, in every
  deployment shape, and never a production market by mistake.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Put a selectable sign source, or any `/lab` route, in the production
  composition (`composition.test.ts`, `labSurface.test.ts` — unchanged).
- Serve the chart in the Lab from `OTC_API_BASE`: in a two-process deployment
  that would draw production's market under the Lab's controls.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. One Lab-composed process answers `/health`, `/catalogue`, a market's history
   and its tick stream, and `/lab/markets`, on one port.
2. With both bases equal, every panel screen shows the Lab banner; with them
   different, only the Lab screen does.
3. The Lab screen shows the selected market's chart from `/labengine`; after a
   push lands, the chart's last price is the landing price.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate.
