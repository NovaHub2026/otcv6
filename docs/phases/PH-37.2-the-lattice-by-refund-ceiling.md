## 1. Objective

Choose every asset's lattice by what it refunds, rebuild the catalogue on it,
and carry the change to a running deployment without moving a price.

## 2. What is built

**The lattice is chosen by measurement** (`asset.ts`). `TARGET_TIE_RATE` is a
quantile of continuous returns and is now only where the search starts;
`MAX_REFUND_RATE` — **5%**, the Human Owner's number — bounds the _realised_
rate, the published integer unchanged between entry and expiry. Eleven
candidate lattices, ×1 to ×32 of the quantile's own, are measured on one walk
over 8,000 thirty-second horizons, and the coarsest under the ceiling wins.
Coarsest rather than nearest: every step below the ceiling is texture given away
for refunds nobody asked to avoid.

**A seam onto a different lattice keeps its price** (`runtime`). A published
price is an integer count of quanta. The personality fingerprint already made a
lattice change a seam, but a seam continues from the last published price, and
that integer read on a lattice twelve times coarser is a twelfth of the price —
EUR/USD would have opened at 1.0128 instead of 1.1631. The checkpoint records
the quantum its price counts in, and the seam re-expresses it: a rounding, so
the price moves by at most half a new quantum, 0.02 pips on EUR/USD.

**The catalogue is rebuilt** with `--relattice`, a flag the builder did not have
because keeping each asset's lattice was the rule PH-34 and PH-35 depended on.

## 3. What it measured

Thirty assets, every one under the ceiling: refunds **3.29% to 4.98%** against
0.1–0.75% before, lattices coarsened ×6 to ×16, and every asset loses one or two
published decimals.

Per asset, and the build that produced it:
[`PH-37-THE-STAIRCASE.md`](../evidence/PH-37-THE-STAIRCASE.md).

## 4. What is re-measured, not retuned

Every pinned number the recalibration moves: the recorded lattice tie rates, the
detection floors, the realism bands, the candle-granularity bands, the Lab's
distance unit and the fast-against-slow tempo separation. Each by its own test's
procedure, with the measurement recorded beside it.
