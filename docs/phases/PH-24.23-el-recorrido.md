## 1. Objective

_"En la tarjeta de empuje, abajo: una casilla de precio y un botón «+». El
objetivo es colocar tramos, máximo cinco, y el precio va a buscar esos puntos
en el orden que marcas, todo con movimiento natural. Colocas los puntos y
activas «Buscar»; hace todos los trayectos y, cuando cumple, se detiene. El
último tramo no depende del cierre de la vela: si quiero que cierre, lo marco
en la casilla de cierre."_

A push moves the price a distance; a close ends the candle somewhere; the
operator also wants a **path** — here, then there, then there — and the
market to walk it as a market would.

## 2. What is built

- **The route.** Under the push rows: a price box, «+» to add it as the next
  point (up to five, each removable), and **«Buscar»**. The points are marked
  on the chart as numbered lines. A click on the chart fills whichever box was
  last focused — the route's or the close's.
- **How the market walks it.** `POST markets/:id/route?points=…&pace=`. On a
  fork of the live market the Lab plays, point by point, the direction of
  sube / baja (PH-24.16: runs for, shorter runs against, from a Lab-only
  stream) at the chosen pace until the price reaches or crosses the point, then
  turns to the next. The signs the fork drew are the script the live market
  plays — the engine's magnitudes and intervals, the Lab's signs, exactly as a
  push — and the Lab knows the sequence at which each point is reached. The
  time each leg takes emerges from the distance and the pace; nothing is tied
  to the candle. A point not reached within the leg's tick budget is named
  («punto 3») and nothing is armed.
- **State, not data.** While the route runs the card says SUBIENDO / BAJANDO
  for the current leg; points already reached dim; «×» cancels the route. When
  the last point is reached the market is free and the list is the operator's
  to run again.
- **Acts do not step on each other.** A route releases whatever was armed (a
  push, a close), recorded; a push or a sube / baja interrupts a running route,
  recorded; «Fijar cierre» is refused while a route runs, as while a push runs,
  because a plan computed on the keystream would not describe the market.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch the price core; the route is signs on a fork and a script, like a push.
- Tie the route to the candle: a close is the Cierre card's act.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. A route of one to five points reaches each point in order — the record's
   price at each point's sequence is at or beyond it in the leg's direction —
   and the market is free after the last.
2. An unreachable point is refused by number; a release mid-route frees the
   market; a push over a route releases it and says so; a close during a route
   is refused.
3. The card takes points, marks them on the chart, runs and cancels the route
   from the panel; the panel flow proves it through the record.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate; a screenshot for the Human Owner.
