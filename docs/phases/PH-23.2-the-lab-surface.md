# PH-23.2 — The Lab Surface: Isolation, State, And What May Never Leave It

## 1. Objective

Give the Lab a surface, and make it impossible for that surface to exist in
production.

PH-23.1 proved the hard part is cheap. This subphase is about the boundary,
because the Lab exposes three things the product must never expose: the engine's
latent state, its keystream cursors, and the ability to choose among futures.

## 2. The boundary is composition, not configuration

ADR-0015 §3, and it is the whole design.

A flag is a thing that can be wrong. `LAB_ENABLED=false` in production is a
claim about an environment variable, and Cycle Audit 7 measured what claims
about flags are worth: a bind default that no test pinned, a portability scan
that skipped the directory its docstring named, a browser suite that reported
passes while launching nothing.

So the Lab is a **module the production composition does not import**. Not
present and disabled — absent. An architecture test asserts it, in the same way
`dependencies.test.ts` asserts that `@otc/web` cannot reach the attack battery.

## 3. What the Lab exposes, and why each is forbidden elsewhere

| Exposed                                           | Why production may not                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Regime, its age, the cascade state, arrival rate  | §8 of the specification. Latent state is not a leak on its own, but it is the input to one.                                                                          |
| **Keystream cursors**                             | INV-010, directly. A cursor plus the key would reconstruct every future price; a cursor alone narrows the search enormously. This is the sharpest thing on the list. |
| Selection among futures                           | INV-001. Choosing a future is not steering the engine, but the _ability_ to choose must not exist where positions do.                                                |
| The engine's step sequence before it is published | It is the future, unsigned.                                                                                                                                          |

## 4. What §10 becomes

The specification asks the Lab to show the engine's directional probabilities —
`UP 51.8% / DOWN 48.2%` with an influence breakdown. **Those numbers cannot
exist here.** The sign is an independent fair coin at every tick and the
magnitude engine cannot observe one, so the value is exactly 50/50, always, and
no influence moves it.

What replaces it is more useful and is the best demonstration of the product the
Lab can offer: the latent **magnitude and rhythm** state, beside a line that says
direction is 50/50 by construction and why. An operator who wants to know
whether the market is exploitable is better served by that sentence and the
battery behind it than by a number that would have to be invented.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. A Lab module exists, serving market state, close control and session record,
   and the production composition does **not** import it. Asserted by an
   architecture test, watched failing against a plant that imports it.
2. No Lab route is reachable from the production application, verified by
   enumerating its routes rather than by reading its configuration.
3. Keystream cursors appear in a Lab response and in no production response,
   asserted by scanning the production controllers.
4. The Lab surface is labelled `OTC LAB` and `SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT` in every
   response that carries Lab data, so a screenshot cannot be mistaken for one of
   the market (§3 of the specification).
5. §10's directional panel reports 50/50 with its reason, and no influence
   breakdown exists to be misread.

## 6. What this subphase does not do

**No UI.** The surface is the API and its isolation. The screen is PH-23.3 and
PH-23.4, and it is worth less than the boundary underneath it.
