## 1. Objective

Directed by the Human Owner on 2026-09-03, before the phase closes: _"mejorar
todo el diseño de UX del Lab y del panel admin del motor completamente,
haciéndolo mejor visualmente, más intuitivo, sin tantas informaciones
innecesarias, y en español; si hay alguna información es mejor hacer un tooltip
con un icono de info y que aparezca allí."_

So: every screen of the panel — Vista, Crear activo, Activos, Lab — **in
Spanish**, with a clear hierarchy, fewer numbers on the surface, and every
explanation moved behind an **ⓘ** that shows it on hover or tap. The screens
built through PH-20–PH-24 were written to prove things; this subphase makes
them something an operator wants to use.

## 2. Principles

1. **Una pantalla, una pregunta.** Each screen answers one question at a glance
   — _¿qué hace el mercado?_, _¿qué he armado?_, _¿cómo acabó?_ — and the rest
   is one click away, not on the page.
2. **Nada explicativo en la superficie.** A sentence that explains rather than
   states goes behind ⓘ. The states themselves stay visible: `ARMADO`,
   `EXACTO`, `COINCIDE`, `INALCANZABLE`.
3. **Español para el operador, inglés para la máquina.** Labels, verdicts,
   buttons and tooltips are Spanish. Identifiers, test ids, the `OTC LAB —
SIMULATION ENVIRONMENT` banner the specification's §3 requires literally,
   asset ids and API field names stay as they are. Long technical notes the API
   returns in English (the battery's notes, the two scenario refusals) appear
   inside tooltips, labelled _detalle técnico_.
4. **Un solo kit.** One set of components — `Section`, `Row`, `Button`,
   `Field`, `Badge`, `Info` — shared by every page, so the panel looks like one
   product.
5. **Nunca un nivel de retículo bajo la palabra precio** (PH-23.5 §6), nunca un
   veredicto sin su resolución o su cuenta (PH-23.5 §4–§5, PH-24.5), nunca las
   dos cronologías mezcladas (§72–§73). The redesign moves text; it does not
   relax a rule.

## 3. What changes, screen by screen

- **Shell.** Menu in Spanish (_Vista_, _Crear activo_, _Activos_, _Lab_ with the
  `SIM` mark), the engine's health as a dot with the detail behind ⓘ.
- **Vista.** The asset list with name, state and one line of character; the
  chart with its timeframes; the status badges in Spanish (_en vivo_, _al día_,
  _hueco_, _sin hospedar_).
- **Crear activo.** The form with its hints behind ⓘ, the archetype picker
  visual, the result as a state rather than a paragraph.
- **Activos.** Table with rename and retire; confirmations short.
- **Lab.** A header strip (market, price, regime, `ARMADO`/`keystream`); the
  sections as tabs — _Mercado_, _Cierre_, _Posiciones_, _Escenarios_, _Calidad_,
  _Sesión_ — each with its controls first and its readings second; every
  caveat behind ⓘ; the banner permanent.

## 4. What this subphase may not do

- Remove or weaken any guard in `labScreen.test.ts`; where the guard read one
  file it now reads every `.tsx` under `lab/`.
- Hide the §3 banner or the outcome lines behind a tooltip — states are
  visible; only explanations move.
- Change any route, any API field, or any rule about what is shown (§2.5).

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. Every visible label, button, verdict and message authored by the panel is
   Spanish; a guard lists the English words that may remain (the banner, ids,
   `SIM`) and fails on any other capitalised English word in JSX text.
2. No explanatory paragraph is rendered inline: a guard counts JSX text nodes
   longer than 140 characters outside an `Info` and requires zero.
3. The `Info` component is one component, used on every page.
4. Both browser suites pass against the redesigned panel with their text
   expectations updated — and the eight panel flows still find their test ids.
5. The Human Owner sees screenshots of every screen.
6. Targeted gate.

## 6. Scope

- `apps/web/src/app/ui/` — the kit; `apps/web/src/lib/es.ts` — the strings.
- `apps/web/src/app/{Nav,layout}.tsx`, `preview/*`, `assets/**`, `lab/*` —
  restructured; `Lab.tsx` split by section.
- `apps/web/src/labScreen.test.ts`, `panel.stat.test.ts`, `lab.stat.test.ts`.

**Excluded:** the engine API's messages (English stays inside tooltips).

## 7. Result and approval

**APPROVED** 2026-09-03, from executed runs:

- **Criteria 1–3** (`panelUx.test.ts`, 4 tests): every `.tsx` under `app/`
  scanned, comment-stripped — no JSX text node over 140 characters outside an
  `Info`, no English UI word rendered as text, one `Info` definition
  (`ui/kit.tsx`) used by every page. Plants watched failing: `Release market`
  as button text; a 200-character Spanish paragraph rendered inline; a second
  `Info` component. The first attempt at each of the first two plants was void —
  one anchor did not apply, and the guard's code filter dropped any text node
  containing a semicolon, which a Spanish sentence may — and both are the
  reason the filter now drops arrows, calls and assignments instead.
- **The Lab's own guards** (`labScreen.test.ts`, 10 tests) survive the split
  into `lab/{Lab,Mercado,Cierre,Posiciones,Escenarios,Calidad,Sesion}.tsx` by
  reading every file under `lab/`; every behavioural anchor from PH-23.5 to
  PH-24.5 is still asserted, in the kit's spelling where the kit renders it.
- **Criterion 4**, both browser suites against the redesign: `lab.stat` 3 of 3
  (101 s), `panel.stat` 8 of 8 (41 s), with their on-screen expectations moved
  to Spanish and to the tabs. Getting there found four things the redesign had
  broken or hidden: the price row lived in a hidden tab (the header strip now
  carries it); "the first button in the plan" was an ⓘ, not a neighbour
  (neighbours carry `lab-close-neighbour`); one live-status variant was still
  English (`live — the forming candle is the record…`); the forming-bucket epoch
  read as debug output (it is a hidden test seam now, and the operator sees a
  time of day).
- **Criterion 5**: ten screenshots delivered to the Human Owner — Vista, Crear
  activo, Activos, and the Lab's six tabs plus an open tooltip.
- **The relative close** the Human Owner asked for — ±1, ±2, ±3 lattice steps
  from the price the market stands at when armed — as `delta=` on the close
  routes (`closeRoutes.test.ts`: target = fork price + delta, the reachable
  parity applied, a fractional delta refused by name) and as six buttons on the
  Cierre tab.
- **Targeted gate**: `format:check → build → typecheck:web → typecheck:config →
lint → test:unit`, exit **0**, 114 files, 2,452 tests, then the two browser
  suites on the same still tree.
