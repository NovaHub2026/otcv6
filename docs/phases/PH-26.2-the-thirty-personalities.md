## 1. Objective

Give each of the thirty assets a personality that is **legal by construction,
distinct by measurement, and itself by design** — without adding an archetype or
a family, which are the Human Owner's decisions.

## 2. What is built

**Seats.** `packages/engine/src/seats.ts` records, for every asset, a narrowing
of its archetype's sampled ranges: where inside `major-fx` EUR/USD lives, where
inside `sector-etf` NVIDIA does. A seat carries the asset's id, display name,
archetype, family, reference price with its dated source, a one-paragraph
character, a dispersion budget, and — when that budget leaves the archetype's
declared band — a note saying so. The personality is not in the seat; it is
**drawn from the seat** under a stream seeded by the asset's own id, by the same
`sampleArchetype` that draws from an archetype.

**The seat guard.** `seats.test.ts` checks every seat the way `families.test.ts`
checks every archetype, and then more:

- inside its parent on every sampled trait and on tail weight;
- feasible at its worst corner, through `assertArchetypeFeasible` itself;
- sixty draws legal and inside the seat;
- its budget departure declared when it departs, and only then;
- **no two seats can draw personalities the differentiation check would
  refuse** — measured over twenty draws per seat and every cross-seat pair,
  against twice `MINIMUM_TRAIT_DISTANCE`, because a refusal at the
  differentiation stage arrives after the expensive calibration.

**Retired ids.** The thirty ids carry `-otc`. `eurusd`, `gbpjpy`, `btcusd`,
`spx` and `xauusd` are retired and never reused: a checkpoint carries no
personality fingerprint, so a state file written by the old personality would be
resumed by the new one under the same name (INV-008, INV-009).

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- **It may not add to `ASSET_ARCHETYPES` or `ASSET_FAMILIES`.** A seat is a
  narrowing, reachable only through its asset; `archetypeById` cannot resolve
  it and the admin surface cannot name it.
- **It may not type a personality.** Every trait value in the catalogue is a
  draw, reproducible from the recorded keyring label.
- **It may not hide a departure.** A budget outside the archetype's band is a
  fact about the asset, recorded next to the number.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. Thirty seats, in the four groups the Human Owner named, every id legal and
   none a retired incumbent's.
2. Every seat passes `assertArchetypeFeasible`, lies inside its parent, and
   draws legally inside itself.
3. Every budget departure is declared, and the departures are exactly the eight
   equities and six thematic indices.
4. The closest cross-seat draw over twenty draws per seat clears twice the
   differentiation floor.
5. Every guard watched failing against a planted defect.
