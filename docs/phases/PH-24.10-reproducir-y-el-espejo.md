## 1. Objective

Sections §74–§77 of the specification — replay, seed, snapshot, engine
comparison — open since PH-23 §10. Everything they rest on exists and is
tested: an engine restores from a snapshot to an identical continuation
(INV-008, INV-009), the mirror test asserts that flipping every sign leaves
every magnitude and interval unchanged (ADR-0003, `mirror.ts`), and settlement
recomputes from the record alone. None of it has a screen. This subphase gives
the Lab two windows onto it: **Reproducir**, which replays a market from a kept
snapshot and checks the replay against what was published, and **el espejo**,
which runs two forks from the same state — the keystream's signs and every sign
flipped — and shows that only the signs differ.

## 2. What is built

- **Kept snapshots.** The Lab keeps the last snapshots of each hosted market —
  one on every arm and release, one a minute otherwise, bounded — as the engine
  snapshot the Lab already reads (cursors included: this is the Lab, INV-010's
  one permitted place).
- **`POST /lab/markets/:id/replay?from=<sequence>`** — restore the kept snapshot
  taken at that sequence into a fork, replay forward to the current sequence, and
  compare every replayed tick with the published record: `replayed`, `identical`,
  the first divergence if any. A divergence would be a finding about the engine
  (INV-009); the route's job is to make one visible, not to hide it.
- **`GET /lab/markets/:id/mirror?ticks=N`** — two forks from the current
  snapshot: one drawing the keystream's signs, one with every sign flipped
  (`SelectableSigns` playing the negation). Returns both paths' summaries and
  the check: identical magnitudes and intervals, mirrored displacements.
- **The Reproducir tab**: the kept snapshots as a list (sequence, instant, why
  it was kept), a replay with its verdict, the mirror with its two paths and the
  sentence that makes it the product's proof.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Publish a snapshot anywhere but the Lab (INV-010).
- Replay into the live engine: replays run on forks; the market is never
  rewound.
- Report a replay as identical without saying how many ticks it compared.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. Snapshots are kept on arm, release and by time, bounded per market.
2. A replay from a kept snapshot over at least two hundred ticks is identical to
   the record, and the route says how many it compared; a planted divergence in
   the comparison is caught.
3. The mirror shows equal magnitudes and intervals and mirrored displacements on
   the shipped engine.
4. The Reproducir tab shows all three; a browser flow replays and reads the
   verdict.
5. Guards, plants, targeted gate.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/api/src/lab/snapshots.ts`, `replay.ts`; `lab.controller.ts`
- `apps/web/src/app/lab/Reproducir.tsx`, `Lab.tsx`, `lib/es.ts`
- tests beside each

**Excluded:** large runs as jobs (PH-24.11).
