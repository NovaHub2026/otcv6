## 1. Objective

Write `docs/reports/IMPROVEMENT-REPORT-001.md`: the case for what to build
next and in what order, grounded in measurements the repository holds or
this phase took, with every engine proposal classed against the mirror test.

## 2. Structure

1. The engine, toward realism — stylised fact by stylised fact, what the
   metrics and the battery say, each mechanism classed _sign-blind by
   construction_ / _sign-blind if done this way_ / _not sign-blind, not for
   this engine_.
2. The trader's screen — what an observer of `apps/web` sees that a venue's
   client would not, and the reverse, with the invariant each proposal keeps.
3. The Lab — from PH-27.4's footprints: what an operator can do, what each
   act costs, what they cannot yet do, classed by what it touches.
4. The order — the debts that are phases and the proposals above, ranked into
   a proposed Cycle 10 with what each would take.

## 3. Acceptance

1. Every proposal names its measurement and its invariant.
2. Every engine proposal is classed against the mirror test.
3. The report ends in a ranked Cycle 10 and changes nothing itself.

## 4. What the report says

[`docs/reports/IMPROVEMENT-REPORT-001.md`](../reports/IMPROVEMENT-REPORT-001.md),
in four parts:

1. **The engine, toward realism.** Eight stylised facts against what the
   engine models, each remedy classed A (sign-blind by construction), B
   (sign-blind if done this way, the mirror test the check) or C (not for
   this engine): session seasonality and family co-movement of _magnitude_
   are A; jumps are B; the leverage effect and any level drift are C and
   named as such so nobody adds them as an improvement. The two archetypes no
   seat uses, and the five fifteen-minute cells the horizon record cannot
   police, with what closing each takes.
2. **The trader's screen.** Seven proposals with the measurement each rests
   on and the invariant each keeps: bounding a told hole, multiplexing the
   charts, a list that scales, a retired market's screen, volume and spread
   panes if the class-A quantities are adopted, ten thousand observers held.
3. **The Lab.** From PH-27.4's footprints, seven proposals classed by what
   they touch — the Lab's surface, the runtime, or the guarantees ADR-0015
   forbids — including the persisted tick record that closes PH-25.1's two
   findings, and the one class that is forbidden.
4. **The order.** Seven phase-sized items ranked, and a proposed Cycle 10 of
   three: the persisted record, the trading boundary, the engine's next
   class-A facts — in that order, and why.
