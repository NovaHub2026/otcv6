## 1. Objective

_"Vamos a agregar al gráfico un cronómetro de cuánto le falta a esa vela para
finalizar; si cambias de timeframe se actualiza también."_

The close is fixed on the candle the chart shows (PH-24.20) and the operator
decides when to act by how much of it is left. The chart said which candle was
forming and not how long it had.

## 2. What is built

- **A countdown on the chart.** In the chart's top-right corner, over the
  candles and out of the way of a click (`pointer-events: none`): the time left
  in the bucket now forming, on the chart's timeframe — `m:ss` under an hour,
  `h:mm:ss` from an hour up. It counts down four times a second and reads
  `0:00` at the boundary before the next bucket takes over.
- **The clock is the market's.** The countdown is anchored on the last tick's
  instant and advanced locally since it was received, so it says what the
  engine's clock says and not what the browser's does; the bucket's end is the
  kernel's own `bucketEnd` on the kernel's own `timeframe`, the alignment every
  candle and every close uses (ADR-0017 inclusive end aside: the countdown is
  to the boundary).
- **It follows the timeframe.** Choosing another timeframe recomputes it at
  once: the 5m countdown exceeds the 1m one by whole minutes, because both
  buckets end on the same second of the minute.
- Shared by the panel and Vista: `PreviewChart` is one component (PH-24.12).

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Read time from the browser's clock as the market's: only the elapsed
  interval since the last tick is local.
- Cover the chart's controls or intercept a click meant for the mark (PH-24.21).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The chart shows a countdown that decreases second by second on the live
   market and formats as §2 says.
2. Switching the chart's timeframe changes it consistently with the buckets'
   shared alignment.
3. Guards, plants, targeted gate; a screenshot for the Human Owner.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/web/src/app/preview/PreviewChart.tsx`, `lib/es.ts`,
  `labScreen.test.ts`, `lab.stat.test.ts`
