## 1. Objective

The Lab arms one market at a time on the screen while the venue hosts five. An
operator who has armed a close on EUR/USD and a scenario on BTC/USD sees only
the one selected; the others' state — armed, pending, landed — is a click away
and out of mind. Item 11 of the inventory of 2026-09-03.

## 2. What changes

- **The asset list carries each market's state**: a badge per market — nothing,
  `ARMADO · N`, or the last outcome (`EXACTO` / `FALLÓ`) — read from one new
  route, `GET /lab/control`, which returns every hosted market's control state at
  once instead of one per request.
- **A `Tablero` tab** (overview): every market in a row — price, regime, armed
  state with signs remaining, last applied target and outcome, open positions —
  so a session across several markets reads as one thing.
- **Release all**: one act, recorded once per market it released.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Add a way to arm several markets with one vector: each market's engine has its
  own coin and its own future; an act is per market. The overview shows, it
  does not batch — except for release, which is safe to batch because it only
  ever returns markets to their keystreams.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /lab/control` returns the control state of every hosted market; the
   asset list shows a badge that changes when a market is armed and when its
   close lands.
2. The Tablero tab shows every market's state and open positions; a browser flow
   arms two markets and reads both rows.
3. `POST /lab/release-all` releases every armed market and records one action
   per market released.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/api/src/lab/lab.controller.ts` — `control` (all), `release-all`
- `apps/web/src/app/lab/{Lab,Tablero}.tsx`, `lib/es.ts`, `labScreen.test.ts`,
  `lab.stat.test.ts`

**Excluded:** replay and snapshot (PH-24.10), large runs as jobs (PH-24.11).

## 6. Result and approval

**APPROVED** 2026-09-03, from executed runs:

- **Criterion 1** — `GET /lab/control` returns every hosted market's control
  state with its price, regime and open positions in one answer; the asset list
  carries a badge per market — `ARMADO · N` while armed, `EXACTO`/`FALLÓ` once
  its last close landed. `closeRoutes.test.ts` (14): on a two-market venue both
  armed, both reported.
- **Criterion 2** — the Tablero tab: one row per market. In Chromium
  (`lab.stat.test.ts`, now 7 flows, 125 s): EUR/USD and GBP/JPY armed from the
  screen, both badges `ARMADO`, both board rows `ARMADO`.
- **Criterion 3** — `POST /lab/release-all` released both, recorded one
  `release` action per market with `all: true`, and released nothing the second
  time; on the screen, one click and both rows read `keystream`, two `liberado ✓`
  in the Lab timeline.
- **What the board may not do**: arm. The guard forbids any close, scenario or
  preset route from `Tablero.tsx`, requires release-all to be disabled with
  nothing armed, and requires the badges; plants watched failing on all three.
- **Targeted gate**: exit **0**, 115 files, 2,467 tests; `panel.stat` 8 of 8.
