## 1. Objective

Police every horizon the product sells to the threshold its payout implies, for
every asset, and record the result as evidence a later reader can re-derive.

## 2. What has to be true

At the 99% promotional payout a trader breaks even at a 50.2513% win rate, so an
edge is worth exploiting at **0.2513 percentage points**. A verdict of "no edge"
means nothing unless the test could have seen an edge that size.

Detecting 0.2513pp at 80% power and 5% significance needs about **310,700**
decided windows. At the 15-minute horizon, non-overlapping, that is roughly nine
simulated years per asset. This subphase targets 350,000 windows — a floor of
0.2368pp, with margin.

## 3. Why this is now a run and not a research problem

PH-11.1 removed the two things that made it one.

**Compute.** The engine produces 730,638 ticks per second; a simulated year costs
about 31 seconds. B-002's "roughly a hundred times the history" is true and reads
as prohibitive, but a hundred times a cheap thing is still cheap.

**The error bar.** If direction outcomes had been overdispersed the way the
lattice tie rate is — four times its binomial variance — then simulating longer
would have bought far less than the arithmetic promised, and every floor already
recorded in the project would have been optimistic. PH-11.1 measured the design
effect at 1 across all eight horizons, with the tie rate as a positive control
reading 4.62 from the same windows.

So what remains is a long run and an honest write-up.

## 4. Design

### 4.1 Streaming, because the dataset would not fit

Nine years of the fastest asset is on the order of 9×10⁸ ticks. An
`ObserverDataset` materialises instants and prices; at that length it is several
gigabytes. `HorizonAccumulator` keeps `O(horizons)` state and never holds the
path.

### 4.2 Contiguous segments, because a venue is one record

PH-11.1 measured the design effect across **independent runs**. A live venue is a
single continuous history, so this measures it across **contiguous segments of
one run** — the setting that actually applies, and the more conservative of the
two, since segments of one path share whatever long-memory structure exists.

100 segments also buys resolution where PH-11.1 had least: the design effect
carries a ±14% relative standard error instead of ±23%, which is what the
10-minute horizon's loose 1.62 reading needs.

### 4.3 The z-score carries the measured dependence

An effective sample size, not a nominal one. Where the design effect is 1 this is
the ordinary binomial z; where it is not, the verdict is stated at the
sensitivity the sample actually has.

### 4.4 The evidence records itself

The run is a deliberate act, not a test — it takes tens of minutes and produces
`docs/evidence/PH-11-HORIZON-COVERAGE.md`. Two things keep it from becoming an
unfalsifiable table of numbers, which is the failure mode PH-10 found in
`MEASURED_LATTICE_TIE_RATES`:

- `horizonCoverage.stat.test.ts` re-runs the same machinery at a fraction of the
  length on every gate, so the method stays checked.
- The same test **parses the recorded document** and re-derives each row's floor
  and payout verdict from its own recorded sample count and design effect. A
  transcription error, a stale row, or a hand-edited "yes" fails the build.

## 5. Scope

- `measureHorizonCoverage` and its report format.
- `horizonEvidence.ts`, run by `npm run evidence:horizons`.
- The recorded evidence document, all five assets.
- The statistical test: method re-run, and the record re-derived from itself.

## 6. Exclusions

- Conditioning. This is the unconditional direction test. It is **not** a
  replacement for the battery and a clean result here is not stronger evidence
  than a clean battery verdict — a leak that needs conditioning to see will not
  appear. Saying so is part of the deliverable.
- Retuning. An edge at any horizon is recorded, not corrected.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

1. Every asset reaches a floor finer than 0.2513pp at every horizon from 30
   seconds to 15 minutes — or the shortfall is stated per asset and horizon.
2. No horizon shows an edge significant after correcting for the 40 tests run.
3. The recorded document re-derives from itself under test.
4. Guardrails, lint, build and the unit suite pass.
