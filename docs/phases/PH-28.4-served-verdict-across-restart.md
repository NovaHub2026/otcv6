## 1. Objective

Close PH-25.1's findings a and c on the product, not on one asset in a test:
a production-composed venue of the thirty, killed with `SIGKILL` and
restarted, whose served record every observer can resume across the kill and
whose candle record has no hole at it — measured by the standing instrument
from outside the process and recorded as evidence.

## 2. What is run

1. Boot `apps/api` in production composition on the thirty with a state
   directory, a tick record and publication on; let it run past a checkpoint.
2. Read every asset's served record from sequence 1 (`readServedRecord`),
   `SIGKILL` the process, restart it on the same directory.
3. For every asset: resume from the sequence the first read ended at — the
   read must not be refused and must tell no gap; the ticks on either side of
   the kill are contiguous; the stored minute bars through the kill are
   contiguous in sequence.
4. `npm run assurance:served` against the restarted venue: the standing
   verdict on a record that spans the kill, with the seam family built.
5. `npm run state:verify` on the directory after the run: consistent.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. Thirty of thirty assets resume across the kill with no gap told.
2. Thirty of thirty have contiguous minute bars through the kill.
3. The standing job's record names the venue and grades every asset; none
   `exploitable`.
4. The evidence is recorded in `docs/evidence/PH-28-DURABLE-VENUE.md` with
   the commit, the composition and the numbers.
