# PH-36.1 — The Reopening, Its Bounds, And What A Broker Sees Of It

---

## 1. Objective

Build [ADR-0020](../decisions/ADR-0020-a-stall-reopens-itself.md): a market
refused by the catch-up bound reopens itself at the clock as a recorded seam,
inside its bounds, visibly, and without touching the bound itself.

## 2. What is built

**`reopenStalledMarket` (`packages/runtime/src/reopen.ts`).** Given a hosted
market and its registered asset, returns a new `HostedMarket` opened at the
clock from the market's last published price, on `startKeyEpoch(instant,
previousEpoch)`, at `lastPublished.sequence + DEFAULT_SEQUENCE_LEASE`, with the
same personality fingerprint and the same bound. It returns the same
`RecoveryOutcome` shape a boot seam returns, so everything downstream that
already understands a seam understands this one. It refuses a market that has
never published.

**`Venue.reopen` (`packages/runtime/src/venue.ts`).** Replaces the hosted market
for one asset id, keeping the registered asset. The venue is a scheduling
convenience, so this is a map write and a refusal for an unknown id.

**The decision (`apps/api/src/venue.service.ts`).** On each pass, a failure
whose error is a `CatchUpTooLargeError` is a candidate. It is reopened when the
market has published since its last automatic reopening and at least
`MIN_REOPEN_INTERVAL_MS` has passed since it; otherwise it stays stalled by
name, as today. Every other failure kind is untouched.

Reopening is not only a swap. In the same act the service:

- tells the feed where its new window begins (`feed.seam`), which is what keeps
  a resuming client from being told it holds ticks nobody wrote;
- seals the commitment chain and starts a new one past the gap
  (`publication.seamChain`), the same treatment `prime` gives a seam found in
  the record at boot;
- updates the market's recovery state, so `GET /markets` says `seam` rather
  than reporting the recovery of a process that ended hours ago;
- clears the stall, logs one line naming the lag, the price carried over and
  the sequence resumed at, and counts it in `otc_market_reopenings_total`.

The record itself needs nothing: it writes a `RecordedSeam` whenever an append
jumps a sequence, which is exactly what the first post-reopening tick does.

**The switch.** `OTC_AUTO_REOPEN=0` restores the previous behaviour.

## 3. How it is verified

- **The runtime unit**: a market driven past its bound by a jumping clock is
  reopened, publishes again, carries its price, jumps its sequence by a lease,
  and lands on a strictly greater key epoch. A market that never published is
  refused.
- **The bounds**: a second reopening inside `MIN_REOPEN_INTERVAL_MS` is
  refused; a reopening with no publication since the last one is refused; a
  publish refusal and a record refusal do not reopen.
- **The venue integration** (`apps/api`): a venue whose clock jumps an hour
  stalls, reopens itself, and serves prices again — with the seam in
  `GET /markets/:id/seams`, the feed's window declared at the new sequence, and
  `settle()` refusing a contract whose window spans the gap.
- **Planted defects**, each watched failing before the guard is believed:
  reopen on any failure kind; reopen without the minimum interval; reopen
  without sealing the chain; reopen at the old key epoch; reopen at the
  checkpoint's instant instead of the clock.

## 4. What it does not do

Nothing in this subphase changes price generation, the catch-up bound, the
catalogue, or the API contract. The engine is untouched; the mirror test and
the battery run because the gate runs them, not because this subphase can move
them.
