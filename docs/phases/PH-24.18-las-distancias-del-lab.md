## 1. Objective

_"Ahora que calibramos los ticks vamos a calibrar las distancias y todo lo
relacionado del Lab para que se adapte; el objetivo es hacer todo lo más
realista posible."_ PH-24.17 made a tick three to four times more frequent
and about half as large. Every Lab control that counts ticks or lattice steps
therefore means less than it did: a `+10` push moves half of what it moved,
a relative close of `±3` lattice steps is invisible at display precision, a
scenario's `net 40` or a shock of `40` is a fraction of a candle, the bias's
runs of 2–6 ticks last a second, and the burst paces — fractions of a base
tempo that is now four times shorter — run at 60–125 ms a tick.

The Lab's controls should be expressed in what an operator sees: **the
candle**. This subphase gives every market one distance unit derived from
itself, and states every distance in it.

## 2. What is built

- **The unit.** `distanceUnit(asset)`: a quarter of the median range of the
  market's 1-minute candle, measured on a fork of the live market over the
  last thirty minutes of its own future (cached for five minutes), in lattice
  steps and as a price. `GET /lab/markets/:id/state` carries it
  (`distance: { unitSteps, unitPrice, candleRangeSteps }`), and the strip
  shows it: «1 = ¼ de vela de 1m ≈ 0,00005».
- **Pushes by distance.** `POST …/push?distance=±N&pace=…`: the Lab walks a
  fork under the pushed signs and the pace's arrivals until the displacement
  reaches `N` units (at most 400 ticks), and arms exactly that many ticks. The
  buttons `−10 … +10` send distances; the landing is still announced and read
  from the record. `ticks=` stays for the API.
- **Relative close, scenarios, target price in units.** The `±1 ±2 ±3` close
  buttons, the scenarios' displacement parameters (`net`, shock `size`), and
  the target-price steps are entered in units and converted to lattice steps
  on the screen (the routes keep lattice steps; parity adjusts on apply).
- **Bias runs in market time.** A run for the direction lasts 1–3 seconds of
  the market's own pace, a run against it strictly shorter — the run lengths
  are the ticks the asset's mean interval puts in that time, never fewer than
  two.
- **Paces relative to the market.** `normal` is the market's arrivals;
  `medio` half its mean interval; `rápido` a fifth — the same feel PH-24.15
  had at the old tempo (~300 and ~120 ms on EUR/USD), derived per asset from
  its mean interval by the same bisection PH-24.16 uses for the first tick.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch a magnitude or an interval's law; add anything to a price.
- Change a route's lattice semantics: units are the screen's and the push's
  convenience, steps remain the record's truth.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The unit is measured on the market's own fork and reported per market.
2. `distance=+2` at rápido lands at least two units away in ≤ 400 ticks; the
   ticks armed are what the fork needed; the response says units, steps and
   ticks.
3. Bias runs span 1–3 s of market pace; paces are per-asset intervals within
   20 % of half and a fifth of the mean interval on the first pushed ticks.
4. The strip and the Cierre / Escenarios tabs show units and their price.
5. Guards, plants, targeted gate (unit + Lab and panel browser suites).
