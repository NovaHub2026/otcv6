# PH-27.1 — The Guards That Cannot Fail: Found By Planting, Fixed By Anchoring

## 1. Objective

Take every guard the project's own records name as unable to fail — a test
that passes by having nothing to say, a docstring claim no test holds, a
threshold written against a constant — and either watch it fail against a
planted defect or make it able to. Nothing is closed by reading: each item
below is re-executed on this tree before it is believed, because the list it
comes from (Cycle Audit 8's remaining-items map, `~/.otc-audit8/`) predates
the closing of that audit and several entries were resolved after it was
written.

## 2. Method

For each candidate: (1) find the guard or its absence in the tree; (2) plant
the defect the guard exists for; (3) run the test; (4) if it stays green,
anchor the guard — on a behaviour, never on a spelling or a constant — and
repeat until the plant fails it by name; (5) restore and re-run. A candidate
that already fails its plant is recorded as **holds** with the plant named,
and nothing else is done to it.
