## 1. Objective

_"1. Al pulsar +10, +1 o sube, la tarjeta marca SUBIENDO en verde; con bajar,
BAJANDO en rojo. 2. Si +10 da 30 ticks y luego pulso +10 rojo, la cantidad de
ticks es la resta de lo que quedaba y lo nuevo — siempre una resta. 3. La tasa
del cierre se escribe o se marca con el ratón en cualquier punto del gráfico. El
signo = significa que la operación termina en el punto exacto de la tasa, aunque
la sobrepase por arriba o por abajo mientras tanto; la flecha arriba, que
termina en cualquier precio por encima; la flecha abajo, lo contrario. El flujo:
selecciona o escribe la tasa, selecciona la referencia, fija el cierre; si se
cancela, todo vuelve a ser."_

Three things the panel did not say or do. The push card gave no sign of the
direction it was holding; two pushes in opposite directions replaced one
another instead of netting; and the close knew one reference — exactly here —
where the operator thinks in three.

On the third, the Human Owner asked whether a live rule — turn sube on when the
price crosses the wrong way, off when it crosses back — would spare "touching
the engine". Recorded in `DECISION-LOG.md` (2026-09-04): neither option touches
the price core; the rule alone cannot guarantee the final tick (bias is a
tendency, and the last stretch would still have to be scripted); and the
smaller change is an acceptance test in the selection that already exists.

## 2. What is built

- **SUBIENDO / BAJANDO.** While a push is playing or sube / baja is held, the
  Empuje card's title carries the direction — green for up, red for down — and
  nothing when the market is free.
- **Pushes net.** A push in the direction of the one running still extends it
  (PH-24.10). A push in the opposite direction is **subtracted**: with `R`
  ticks remaining and `N` asked, `N > R` reverses by `N − R` ticks, `N < R`
  shortens the running push to `R − N`, `N = R` frees the market. The response
  says so (`netted: { previousRemaining, applied }`), the session records it,
  and `pushing` reports the direction that survived.
- **The mark.** The close's price is typed, or picked with one click anywhere
  on the chart: the chart names the price at that height, the panel snaps it to
  the nearest lattice level (`lattice.ts`) and marks it with a line and the word
  «cierre». Typing marks it too; clearing the box clears the line.
- **The reference.** `= ▲ ▼` are the close's **condition**, three windows:
  exactly at the mark; any price above it; any price below it. The candle may
  cross the mark meanwhile — what is fixed is where it ends. Flow: mark, choose
  the reference, «Fijar cierre»; «×» cancels and everything is as before.
- **How ▲ and ▼ are honoured.** `close?condition=above|below` (default
  `exact`). The Lab reads the window on a fork as it does for an exact close —
  now keeping the natural signs — and, if the market's own path already ends
  on the asked side, arms exactly that path (`natural: true`, zero attempts):
  the market does what it was going to do. Otherwise `selectCloseWhere`
  (`@otc/engine`) samples sign vectors and takes the first whose close
  satisfies the condition — uniformly from the satisfying closes, so the
  endpoint is not glued to the mark. Impossibility is said without sampling
  when no attainable close is on that side. Parity never applies to a side.
  The record keeps the level the path ends on; the outcome is read as before.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch the price core: `packages/engine`'s market model is unchanged; the
  selection lives beside the exact one and is used only by the Lab.
- Steer the endpoint: the accepted path is a rejection sample, never a
  constructed one (§28, §70).

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The Empuje card shows SUBIENDO / BAJANDO in colour while a push or a held
   direction is in force, and nothing when the market is free.
2. Opposite pushes net as §2 says, in all three cases, and the record says so.
3. A click on the chart fills the box with a lattice level and marks it; the
   three condition windows exist; with ▲ the candle closes above the mark, with
   ▼ below, with = on it; × cancels and removes the mark.
4. `selectCloseWhere` is tested for satisfaction, spread of endpoints, refusal
   without sampling, and the empty window.
5. Guards, plants, targeted gate; a screenshot for the Human Owner.
