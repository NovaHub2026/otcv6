## 2. A battery tests the hypotheses it contains

Every temporal family conditioned on the clock's _phase_ — where in a minute, an
hour, a fifteen-minute grid. None conditioned on the clock's **identity**. An
auditor keyed each 60-second block's direction to a bit of
`splitmix32(blockIndex)`, arithmetic anyone can recompute, and the full battery
returned 70 hypotheses, zero exploitable, worst z 2.26.

Three families now condition on a digest of the block index, at 15s, 60s and
300s. Rebuilding that record: caught at **z = 5.6**, verdict `exploitable`, while
a control built the same way with no bias stays at zero.
`blockIdentity.stat.test.ts` keeps both, and the control matters as much as the
leak — a family that fires on everything is not a detector.

**The general limit is now written down rather than left to be found again.**
`clean` means _no hypothesis in this family set fired at this power_, never
_there is no leak_: an adversary may key on any public function of the record and
no finite battery enumerates them all. What carries the unconditional claim is
ADR-0003 — the sign is an independent fair coin drawn from a stream the magnitude
process cannot observe — and the battery is evidence that the implementation
matches the theorem, not the source of it.

## 3. `clean` was judged against the caller's payout

`classifyStanding` read `sufficientForPayout`, which the battery computes against
whatever payout it was handed. `PAYOUT_TYPICAL = 0.85` is a payout this
repository ships: 33 hours of a fair walk, judged at 0.85, reported **clean** at
a 4.040pp floor — sixteen times coarser than the product margin.

`PRODUCT_MARGIN_PP` was defined in that module, exported, asserted in one test
for its numeric value, and read by nothing. It is read now.

## 4. A refusal that could not fire, removed rather than moved

`assertIndependentFamilies(withheld, [...built])` derived `available` from
`families`, so `missing` was always empty; an auditor deleted the call and the
whole laboratory suite stayed green.

Composition at that point is `[...ATTACK_FAMILIES, ...withheld]` and cannot lose
a family, so a check that it did not is **decoration** — and decoration in a
guardrail is worse than nothing, because it is cited as evidence. What protects
the verdict is the rule that a withheld family which could not be _built_ forces
`undecided`, and that rule is tested. `assertIndependentFamilies` stays exported
for a caller that assembles its own set, where the two lists are genuinely
independent.

## 5. The limiter, and a fix that was wrong twice

PH-16.3 threaded an `EntryResolver` through `exposureByEvent`, `assessBookRisk`
and `ExposureBook` — and stopped one file short of `admit()` and `breaches()`,
which are the two functions a venue actually calls. Cycle Audit 5's construction
still worked against them unchanged.

The first fix here silently admitted all 200 again: it matched an event by its
instants, and an `EventExposure` carries the **first** contract of its group
while the grouping happens on resolved ones. `EventExposure` publishes its
grouping key now.
