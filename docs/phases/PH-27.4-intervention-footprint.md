# PH-27.4 — The Footprint Of A Lab Intervention, Measured On The Record It Wrote

Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL DOCUMENT
Identifier: PH-27.4
Parent: [PH-27](PH-27-review-and-improvement.md)
Status: PLANNED
Created: 2026-09-05

---

## 1. Objective

_"¿Has realizado tests para definir el impacto que tiene el Lab en un activo
cuando lo ejecuta?"_ — measure what an intervention did to the record, not
only what it cannot touch.

## 2. What is measured

For each kind of intervention — a push, a close, a sustained direction to its
ceiling — on a Lab-composed engine from one snapshot, against the
continuation the keystream would have produced from the same snapshot
(reconstructible because the cursor did not move, `selectableSigns.test.ts`):

1. **Displacement** — price at release against the uncontrolled price, in
   lattice steps and in candles moved.
2. **Detectability** — the realism metrics and the battery over the
   controlled segment against the same window uncontrolled: does an
   observer's instrument see it, at what size.
3. **Decay** — ticks after release until the two paths are statistically
   indistinguishable; for a sign-only substitution this should be zero, and
   the measurement says whether it is.

## 3. What is built

`footprint(snapshot, intervention)` in `packages/lab` (pure: two
continuations, the three numbers), a statistical test that runs it for the
three kinds and records the figures, and the numbers on the Lab's session
record (`/lab/session`) so an operator sees the cost of what they pressed.

## 4. Acceptance

1. The three figures for the three kinds, recorded in
   `docs/evidence/PH-27-INTERVENTION-FOOTPRINT.md` with the runs.
2. Decay measured, not assumed: a plant that makes the magnitude path see the
   sign (the leverage effect, ADR-0003) must show a non-zero decay.
3. The session record carries the footprint; the panel shows it.
