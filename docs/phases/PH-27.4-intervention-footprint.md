## 1. Objective

_"¿Has realizado tests para definir el impacto que tiene el Lab en un activo
cuando lo ejecuta?"_ — measure what an intervention did to the record, not
only what it cannot touch.

## 2. What is measured

For each kind of intervention — a push, a close, a sustained direction to its
ceiling — on a Lab-composed engine from one snapshot, against the
continuation the keystream would have produced from the same snapshot
(reconstructible because the cursor did not move, `selectableSigns.test.ts`):

1. **Displacement** — price at release against the uncontrolled price, in
   lattice steps and in candles moved.
2. **Detectability** — the realism metrics and the battery over the
   controlled segment against the same window uncontrolled: does an
   observer's instrument see it, at what size.
3. **Decay** — ticks after release until the two paths are statistically
   indistinguishable; for a sign-only substitution this should be zero, and
   the measurement says whether it is.

## 3. What is built

`footprint(snapshot, intervention)` in `packages/lab` (pure: two
continuations, the three numbers), a statistical test that runs it for the
three kinds and records the figures, and the numbers on the Lab's session
record (`/lab/session`) so an operator sees the cost of what they pressed.

## 4. Acceptance

1. The three figures for the three kinds, recorded in
   `docs/evidence/PH-27-INTERVENTION-FOOTPRINT.md` with the runs.
2. Decay measured, not assumed: a plant that makes the magnitude path see the
   sign (the leverage effect, ADR-0003) must show a non-zero decay.
3. The session record carries the footprint; the panel shows it.

## 5. What was measured

Recorded in [`PH-27-INTERVENTION-FOOTPRINT.md`](../evidence/PH-27-INTERVENTION-FOOTPRINT.md)
from the run's own output, on the fastest tape after a simulated hour, five
thousand ticks past release:

| Intervention                   | Displacement            | Divergent increments | Instants identical | Decay | Offset after the horizon |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------ | ----- | ------------------------ |
| push +10                       | 30 steps, 0.17 candles  | 3 of 5,010           | yes                | 0     | 30                       |
| close, 40-tick selected vector | 2 steps, 0.01 candles   | 18 of 5,040          | yes                | 0     | 2                        |
| bias up, 200 ticks             | 252 steps, 1.44 candles | 79 of 5,200          | yes                | 0     | 252                      |

The answer to the Human Owner's question, in the record's own numbers: an
intervention moves the **level**, by the sum of the signs it changed times
the engine's own magnitudes, and moves **nothing else** — not one instant, not
one increment after release, no personality, no calibration. The
displacement is permanent because the process is driftless: nothing pulls the
price back. Detectability is bounded by the controlled stretch itself.

**Decay is measured, not written in.** `footprint.test.ts` runs the same
comparison on a walker whose variance responds to the signed return — the
leverage recursion of the planted `leverageEffect` fixture — and the decay
there is non-zero, as it must be for a magnitude path that saw the sign. On
the shipped engine it is zero because the magnitude path cannot see one
(ADR-0003), and the figure is what says so.

**On the session record.** Every push records and returns
`footprint: { displacementSteps, naturalLevel }` — the landing against a bare
fork of the same tick count — and the session screen's line for the act
shows it ("huella ±N pasos", `lab-session-footprint`); the push card of the
older `Empujar` screen shows it beside the landing too. The first browser run
looked for it on the controls screen, which shows no announcement by design
(PH-24.20) — the assertion now reads the session screen, where the record is.
A footprint reported against the landing itself (displacement zero) is caught
by `pushRoutes.test.ts`, which walks the bare fork too; watched failing.
