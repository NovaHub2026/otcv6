## 1. Objective

_"¿Podemos tener varios selectores de velocidad — normal, medio (la mitad del
actual), rápido (el nuevo) — para cambiar dependiendo del momento?"_ The
DECISION-LOG entry for PH-24.13 ended with exactly this: the burst's pace as a
control is `BURST_DIVISOR` as a per-push parameter with the same fences. Three
paces, chosen on the strip and applied to the pushes that follow:

| Pace     | Arrival draws for the pushed ticks               | Interval on EUR/USD | Tick size |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | --------- |
| `normal` | the keystream's own (no arrival script)          | the market's        | 100 %     |
| `medio`  | `u = e^(-1/6)` — one sixth of the base tempo     | ~0.4–0.7 s          | ~65 %     |
| `rapido` | `u = e^(-1/12)` — one twelfth (PH-24.13's burst) | ~0.2–0.35 s         | ~55 %     |

All three retract the pending tick and wake the scheduler: the push always
begins at the click. Magnitudes are never touched; the tick sizes above are
duration coupling's, as they would be for any tick that fast.

## 2. What is built

- `POST /lab/markets/:id/push?ticks=±N&pace=normal|medio|rapido` (default
  `rapido`, PH-24.13's behaviour). `normal` arms no arrival script; the landing
  fork plays the same. Extending a push carries the earlier ticks' draws and
  applies the new pace to the new ticks. The pace is recorded in the action.
- The strip: `ritmo · normal | medio | rápido` beside the buttons; the chosen
  pace is sent with every push and shown in the landing line.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch a magnitude, or produce an interval the engine's own law could not.
- Change the market's pace outside a push.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. On a Lab-composed venue, `pace=normal` produces intervals equal to an
   unpushed venue's from the same keyring for the pushed stretch; `medio` at
   most `base / 6`; `rapido` at most `base / 12`.
2. The pace is in the session action and in the strip's landing line.
3. From the screen, each pace can be chosen and a push lands.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate.
