## 1. Objective

Three things the specification asks for that
[LAB-SPECIFICATION-AUDIT-001](../audits/LAB-SPECIFICATION-AUDIT-001.md) found
absent and PH-24.1–24.4 did not build:

- **§70, terminal-convergence diagnostics over controlled candles** (K11,
  LA-05). Selection leaves no signature in a path — that is a theorem about the
  construction, and a plant that replaced the sampler with a constructive solver
  was caught. What no test measures is the other place a pattern can live: **the
  operator's choices**. A Lab session that always closes one tick past entry
  has a distribution of closes no natural market has, and the Lab should be
  able to say so about its own session.
- **§37, the `NON-NATURAL TEST` mode** (M7–M8, LA-06). A target outside the
  natural range is refused today, correctly. The specification also asks for a
  way to reach it anyway, for stress testing. §3 explains why that is answered
  **no** — the one shape ADR-0015 §3 permitted turns out to have no place to go
  inside the Lab that does not break INV-002 or INV-003 there.
- **§72–§73, the engine-event timeline fed** (LA-03 in part). `LabSession` has
  two streams by construction and the engine's has never received an event. The
  regime and cascade phase are on the state panel; their _transitions_ belong in
  the timeline beside the operator's acts, so a session reads as evidence.

## 2. §70 — a diagnostic over the session's closes, not over paths

The paths are the engine's; the closes are the operator's. The diagnostic
therefore runs over the **Lab actions** in the session: for every applied close
that has passed its instant, the signed distance from entry (or from the price
when armed) to the target, in lattice steps. It reports:

- how many controlled closes the session holds, and how many landed exactly
  (`control`'s outcome, read the way settlement reads);
- the distribution of signed distances — count at −1, 0, +1, and beyond;
- the **fraction that sit exactly one step past the entry in the position's
  winning direction**, against the fraction a fair coin would produce, with the
  number of closes the comparison rests on. Two closes tell nothing and the
  panel says so; the resolution travels with the verdict (PH-23.3 §3, CA7-05).

It is served at `GET /lab/session/closes` and rendered beneath the session's
timelines. It never touches the engine, and it is a diagnostic about the
operator — which is what §70 is actually asking for, once the paths are known
to carry nothing.

## 3. §37 — the non-natural terminal tick is not built, and this is why

ADR-0015 §3 permits the synthetic tick "only where it can never enter a
published record". Building it made the condition concrete, and it cannot be
met inside the Lab either.

The Lab market's feed **is** the Lab's published record: its chart draws from
it, its positions settle against it, `control` reads outcomes from it. A
synthetic tick has two places to go, and both are wrong:

- **Into the feed.** The engine is the feed's single writer (ADR-0012) and
  numbers its own ticks; a tick appended from outside collides with the
  sequence the engine's next tick will carry, and every observer resuming from
  that sequence sees two different ticks under one number — INV-002 broken, in
  the Lab, on purpose.
- **Beside the feed**, as a settlement-only overlay the chart never sees. Then
  a position settles at a price no chart showed, which is the one thing L6–L7
  and INV-003 say may never happen — and a Lab that lies to itself about the
  price in force is not a testing environment for a product whose whole claim
  is that it does not.

So the specification's M7 is answered **no, by design**, and the Lab's stress
path stays what PH-24.2 built: an unreachable target is refused with the
reason, the reachable neighbours are named, and the operator can widen the
window — the next candle, a longer timeframe, an expiry further out — until
selection reaches it. That is stress testing of the market as it is, not of a
market with a tick painted onto it. Recorded in `DECISION-LOG.md`.

## 4. §72–§73 — feeding the engine timeline

`VenueService` already knows when a market stalls and recovers, and the engine's
snapshot exposes the regime and the cascade phase. A Lab observer records, per
asset, on each tick: a regime change (`compressed → elevated`), a cascade phase
change, a stall and its recovery, a seam at resume. `LabSession.recordEvent` has
been waiting for it since PH-23.4. The screen's left column stops saying
"nothing recorded yet".

## 5. What this subphase may not do

- Append a tick to any feed from outside the engine, in the Lab or anywhere.
- Report a §70 verdict without the count it rests on.
- Merge the two timelines, or let a Lab action into the engine's.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /lab/session/closes` reports the distribution of the session's
   controlled closes with its count, and says "too few to say" below a stated
   floor; a session of ten `win-minimum` presets is reported as one-sided.
2. `nonNatural=1` on `close` is answered 400 with §3's reason, and no route can
   append a tick to a Lab feed — asserted by reading the Lab's own sources for
   `feed.publish`.
3. Regime and cascade transitions, stalls, recoveries and seams appear in the
   engine timeline, and no Lab action ever does (the PH-23.4 guard, still).
4. The screen shows the closes diagnostic and the fed timeline; the browser
   test reads a regime or cascade event in the engine column after a scenario
   has been applied and the market has run.
5. Targeted gate.
