# PH-25.2 — The Battery On The Served Record, Across A Restart And A Resume

## 1. Objective

Run the adversarial battery on a record that came over a socket from a spawned
`apps/api`, and prove the instrument sees through the wire: a served record
built to leak is called `exploitable`, and the venue's own served record is not.

## 2. What is built

**`servedAssurance(record, at, options)`** in `packages/lab` — the standing
verdict (`runStandingAssurance`) applied to a `ServedRecord`: the ticks are the
record's ticks, the seam indices come from the record's own discontinuities,
and nothing is read that the wire did not carry. The verdict names what it
could not build.

Three tests:

1. **Through the wire, a leak is still a leak.** The unit fake serves a
   Thue–Morse record — every sign the parity of its block index — and the
   verdict is `exploitable`. The same frames with fair signs are not.
2. **The venue's served record.** A spawned service, the fastest tape, a
   record the length a test can afford; the verdict is `clean` or `undecided`
   and never `exploitable`, every horizon reports its detection floor, and the
   floor is recorded rather than asserted — at three thousand ticks the
   battery cannot see a product-margin edge, and the verdict must say so.
3. **Across a restart.** The record read before a SIGKILL and the record read
   after the resume, joined as one dataset with the seam index where the
   observer recorded it; the withheld seam family is built and named in
   `families`, and the verdict is not `exploitable`.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. A planted leak served over the fake wire is `exploitable`; watched.
2. The real venue's served record is never `exploitable` at the sizes read.
3. A restart seam reaches the battery as a seam family, from the record's own
   discontinuities and nothing else.
4. Every verdict carries its detection floor per horizon, and the evidence
   file records the floors measured at the sizes read.
