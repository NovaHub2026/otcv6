## 1. The variance was short by a factor of two

`assessBookRisk.standardDeviation` computed variance as `(netExposure/2)²`, on
the premise — stated in a comment — that "the operator's outcome on an event
swings by `net` between the two resolutions".

It does not. An up contract costs `stake·r` when the market rises and earns
`stake` when it falls, so the swing is `|C−P|·(1+r)` while `netExposure` is
`r·|C−P|`. Short by `(1+r)/r`: **2.01× at the 99% payout**, in the dangerous
direction, on the operator's headline spread number.

The module was internally inconsistent about it. `expectedProfit` already knew
the two resolutions were different sizes, and `ruin.ts` states them separately.

**Fixed** by accumulating both resolutions directly during grouping, which is
also the only form that survives mixed payout ratios within one event: once the
ratios differ, `C` and `P` cannot be recovered from the obligations.

### The test that could not see it

`matches a simulation of the same book` simulated `e.netExposure / 2` — the
model's own quantity. It validated the model against itself and went on passing
while the number was half what it should be.

It now resolves each event with a coin and pays the contracts out by the same
arithmetic `settle.ts` uses. Both moments are checked; the old form could not
check the mean at all, because a fair coin over `net/2` has mean zero by
construction.

One correction to the new test, worth recording because it was my error and not
the auditor's: it first asserted `mean / expectedProfit` close to 1. At a spread
of ~755 over 20,000 trials the sample mean carries a standard error of ~5.3
against an expectation of ~24 — a 22% relative error. The assertion failed on
ordinary noise. It now compares the mean against its own standard error, which
is the only form that means anything.

## 2. One millisecond of jitter was not two hundred bets

Events were keyed on the raw `entryInstant`, but `settle` resolves entries with
`priceAtOrBefore` — so every contract inside one tick interval is the **same
comparison**. Median tick spacing on `eurusd` is 1.1 seconds.

Two hundred contracts entered a millisecond apart inside an 11.4-second gap
produced one entry tick, one expiry tick and one outcome, and were reported as
**200 effective bets**, accepted in full by the limiter at a peak of 99 against
a limit of 500 — while the true single-comparison obligation was 39.6× the
limit. The identical book without jitter was capped at five contracts.

Reporting 200 where the truth is 1 is precisely the number PH-13 exists to
produce.

**Fixed** with an `EntryResolver` threaded through `exposureByEvent`,
`assessBookRisk` and `ExposureBook`. It is optional only because a book assessed
offline has no record to resolve against, and refusing outright would break the
analyses the model was built for. A venue has the record and must pass one.

## 3. Retention deleted what a dispute still needed

`journalIsPruneable` had no horizon term. A settlement is disputable while its
_expiry_ is inside the window; re-deriving it needs the _entry_ tick, up to one
contract horizon earlier. That left a rolling band one horizon wide of
settlements whose expiry was inside the window and whose entry price had been
deleted — INV-009 broken by the only code here that removes anything.

`now` was also unvalidated, in that same code, while the input beside it was
hardened with a comment explaining why. `now = Infinity` pruned everything.

**Fixed.** Retention reaches back `disputeWindowMs + longestHorizonMs`, and both
`journalIsPruneable` and `partitionForRetention` refuse an unusable clock.

## 4. B-018 is deferred, with a reason

The guardrail blind spots are a set, not a defect, and two of them are
structural rather than missing patterns:

- **Computed-property access defeats every rule in `sourceScan.ts`.**
  `Date['now']()` reaches the matcher as `Date[""]`, because the stripper
  replaces string literals before matching. No regex over stripped text can see
  a computed property name, so this is not fixable by adding patterns — it needs
  a parser.
- **The scan roots are a hand-maintained constant**, so `packages/lab`, `tools/`
  and `apps/` are unscanned and any new package is unscanned by default.
  Widening them needs an allowlist first: `tools/sim/src/horizonEvidence.ts`
  already calls `Date.now()` four times for elapsed-time logging.

Both deserve their own subphase rather than a patch at the end of this one. The
half that _was_ structural and cheap — the follower guard's string-unaware
comment stripper and its name-chasing — was fixed in PH-16.2, because it was
load-bearing for INV-002.
