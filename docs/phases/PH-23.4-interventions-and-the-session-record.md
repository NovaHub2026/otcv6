## 1. Objective

The rest of the specification: interventions (§11–§18), scenarios (§48–§49), the
event and intervention timelines (§72–§73), and the logging that makes every
experiment auditable (§78).

## 2. Interventions are selection too

§13 asks for "Bullish Pressure · Medium · 30s". §48 asks for a `Bull Trend →
Pullback`. Both read as steering, and neither has to be.

PH-23.1 established the mechanism: the magnitudes are the engine's and the signs
are a fair coin, so **any sign vector is the engine's own output**. A bullish
intervention is then a _selection criterion over sign vectors_ rather than a
bias applied to the engine:

| Specification                      | As a selection criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| §13 bullish pressure, medium, 30 s | among natural continuations of the next 30 s, keep one whose net displacement is positive by a stated amount                                                                                                                 |
| §14 increased volatility           | keep one whose realised range is in the upper part of its own distribution                                                                                                                                                   |
| §15 shock                          | ~~keep one containing a displacement above a threshold~~ — **not selectable** (LA-01): a shock is a magnitude event and the signs cannot produce one; the Lab locates the engine's next large step and selects its direction |
| §48 bull trend → pullback          | keep one whose shape matches: sustained rise, then a counter-move of a stated depth                                                                                                                                          |
| §16 target price, medium strength  | keep one that reaches a region, without requiring an exact terminal value                                                                                                                                                    |

**The engine still decides the path**, which is the sentence §49 and §81 both
insist on, and here it is literally true: the Lab is choosing among things the
engine produced, not producing anything.

And the same honesty falls out as in PH-23.1: a criterion nothing satisfies is
reported as a measured acceptance rate of zero, not as a failure to steer hard
enough.

## 3. What the session record must carry

§78 requires every intervention to record its timestamp, asset, engine version,
action, parameters, initial state, resulting state, outcome and diagnostics.
§72–§73 require **natural engine behaviour and Lab actions to be shown
separately and never mixed**, which is the part that matters: a timeline that
blended them would make a Lab session unreadable as evidence about the engine.

So the record has two streams by construction, not by a display filter.

## 4. What this subphase may not do

**It may not let a selection escape the Lab.** Everything here chooses among
futures, and the ability to choose must not exist where positions do — the same
composition boundary PH-23.2 built, extended to cover these routes.

**It may not let a non-natural stress path into a measurement.** §37's synthetic
terminal tick, if it is ever used, is labelled `NON-NATURAL TEST` and excluded
from every realism and quality figure (ADR-0015 §3).

**It may not present an unsatisfied criterion as a weak intervention.** Zero
acceptances means the market does not do that; saying so is the product working.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. An intervention is expressed as a criterion over sign vectors, and applying
   one returns the selected continuation plus the measured acceptance rate.
2. A criterion nothing satisfies reports a rate of zero and says what it looked
   for, rather than returning a best effort.
3. The session record separates engine events from Lab actions structurally, and
   a guard asserts a Lab action can never appear in the engine stream.
4. Every intervention entry carries the fields §78 lists.
5. The isolation guard covers the new routes.

## 6. Result

`selectContinuation` takes a predicate over natural continuations and returns
one that satisfies it, with the measured acceptance rate. `INTERVENTIONS` names
the specification's criteria as predicates: bullish and bearish pressure,
expanded and compressed volatility, the direction of a coming shock, touching a
level, and
`trendThenPullback` expressed as a shape with the pullback after the peak.

`LabSession` keeps two arrays with two element types. Not one array with a
`source` field — a field can be set wrong, a type cannot be.

## 7. The guard that took three tries

An intervention that gives up and pushes is the failure this subphase exists to
prevent, and it **survived every test written for it**: a sampler that draws
honestly for fifty attempts and then forces the signs it needs passed all six,
because each criterion was satisfied long before the patience ran out.

The test that catches it asks for a net displacement inside a two-step band at
40% of the total. Measured on the fixture: 830 draws — far past any patience
limit — and satisfiable only by a **mixed** vector, because an all-positive path
lands five times higher. A pushing sampler never satisfies it.

Two other guards were tightened for the same reason in a different direction:
one forbade the _word_ `lab` in `app.module.ts` and fired on the comment
explaining why an export is safe; another forbade `merged` anywhere in
`session.ts` and fired on the sentence explaining why there is no merged
timeline. A guard that punishes the explanation of its own rule is one that gets
deleted the first time it is inconvenient. Both check what the code does now.

## 8. Acceptance

1. **Met.** An intervention is a predicate; applying one returns the selected
   continuation and the measured rate.
2. **Met.** An unsatisfiable criterion reports zero and returns nothing. Watched
   failing against a plant that returned a best effort.
3. **Met.** Two streams, two types, no merged accessor. Watched failing.
4. **Met.** Every §78 field is required rather than optional, asserted.
5. **Carried.** The intervention routes are not yet wired into the Lab
   controller; the mechanism and its record are. Wiring is a view onto them and
   is worth less than the boundary underneath.

## 8.1 Corrected after the fact

**LAB-SPECIFICATION-AUDIT-001, LA-01** (2026-09-03). `INTERVENTIONS.shock` was
not a selection: a displacement's absolute value is the step, the steps do not
depend on the signs, so the predicate accepted the first vector drawn or none
— executed, under five seeds each. It is now `nextShock`, a detector named as
one, with `INTERVENTIONS.directionAt` to choose the sign of the step the engine
is about to produce. A guard now exercises every entry of `INTERVENTIONS` and
requires each to accept some sign vectors and reject others.
