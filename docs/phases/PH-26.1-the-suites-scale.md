## 1. Objective

Make the project's own verification executable against a catalogue of thirty,
before a single one of the thirty is written.

This is not preparation. It is the phase's only genuine unknown: everything else
in PH-26 is authored data, and this is the part where the repository either
survives its own growth or stops being able to answer the question it exists to
answer. A gate that cannot be run is not a gate (`GOVERNANCE.md` §68), and at
thirty assets the gate as written cannot be run.

## 2. The measurements this rests on

Sixteen statistical files reference `ASSET_CATALOGUE`. Nine do work per asset and
seven pin `ASSET_CATALOGUE[0]` or a `.slice(0, 2)` and do not grow. At five
assets the per-asset work is **188.7 million simulated ticks**, and it is not
evenly spread:

| Test                        | Work per asset                                | Ticks at 5 | At 30 |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ----- |
| `multiAsset.stat.test.ts`   | 46 simulated days **plus a full battery**     | 78.3 M     | 470 M |
| `latticeTies.stat.test.ts`  | 12 replicates × 8,000 horizons × 30 s         | 55.7 M     | 334 M |
| `catalogue.stat.test.ts`    | `calibrateAssetAsync`, 10 days × 3 replicates | 50.1 M     | 301 M |
| everything else that scales | six files                                     | 4.6 M      | 28 M  |

The statistical suite ran **4,326 s** on 2026-09-04 at five assets. The three
tests above are the dominant term, and multiplying them by six puts the suite
between six and seven hours of **single-core** wall clock — the suite runs
serially by design (`--no-file-parallelism`, CA6-01), so a larger machine does
not shorten it. Hosted CI measures about 1.5× local against a **180-minute job
ceiling** already raised once this cycle.

Three smaller things fail earlier and harder than the clock does:

- **Six per-asset-id tables are indexed with non-null assertions** under
  `it.each` over `ASSET_CATALOGUE`: `MEASURED_LATTICE_TIE_RATES` (`asset.ts`),
  `GRAIN` (`recalibration.test.ts`), and `PH4_TICK_RMS`, `PH4_MEAN_INTERVAL_MS`,
  `GRAIN_FACTOR`, `PH4_EXCESS_KURTOSIS`, `REALISM_WINDOW_SECONDS`, `PH4_CARRIED`
  (`catalogue.test.ts`). A new id is not slow; it is `undefined` dereferenced or
  `NaN` compared.
- **Two differentiation thresholds are absolute and tied to N = 5**
  (`multiAsset.stat.test.ts`: `shape.accuracy > 0.32`, `rhythmOnly.accuracy >
0.35`). Classifier chance is 1/5 = 0.20 today and 1/30 = 0.033 at thirty, so
  the same numbers become a **six times harder** test of the same property —
  they would fail on a catalogue that is more differentiated, not less.
- **`horizonCoverage.stat.test.ts` asserts recorded rows == assets × 8** against
  `docs/evidence/PH-11-HORIZON-COVERAGE.md`. Thirty assets needs 240 rows, and
  the rows are measurements.

## 3. What is built

1. **A stratified sample, named once and used by all three.** A single exported
   helper decides which assets a per-asset statistical test measures: every
   archetype represented, a recorded stratum, a deterministic draw from a fixed
   seed so the same assets are measured on every run and on every machine. The
   sample is a constant of the suite, not a property of the run.
2. **Every sampling test says what it did not measure.** The printed line names
   the measured assets and the count of unmeasured ones, in the test output and
   in the recorded evidence. §68 is about claims, and "the catalogue reproduces
   its tie rates" is a different claim from "six of thirty do".
3. **The six tables stop being tables.** Each becomes a lookup that refuses by
   name — a missing entry fails with the asset id and the table's own name, not
   with `undefined`.
4. **The two thresholds become multiples of chance**, with the identical-
   personality control as the floor they are measured against, so they mean the
   same thing at five assets and at thirty.
5. **`horizonCoverage`'s evidence is regenerated** at whatever the catalogue is,
   by the runner that produces it, rather than hand-extended.

## 4. What this subphase may not do

- **It may not reduce what is verified without saying so.** A sample is a
  reduction; an unrecorded sample is a lie about coverage.
- **It may not touch the price path.** Nothing here is in `packages/engine`'s
  generation code; INV-001 and INV-006 are not reachable from a test harness.
- **It may not change a recorded constant to make a test pass.** The five
  incumbents' constants stay exactly as measured until PH-26.3 replaces the
  assets that own them.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. The statistical suite, at the current five-asset catalogue, produces the same
   verdicts it produced on 2026-09-04 — the sampling machinery is inert when the
   catalogue is no larger than the sample, and says so in its output.
2. The cost of each heavy suite is bounded by the sample size and not by the
   catalogue: the sample is fixed at five, pinned to the measurement that
   justifies it, and a constant that quietly shrinks fails a named test. The
   measured wall clock at thirty assets is PH-26.3's, taken on the catalogue
   that will actually ship rather than on a synthetic one.
3. Every sampling suite names its unmeasured assets in its output.
4. An asset id absent from any of the six tables fails with a message naming the
   id and the table.
5. The two differentiation thresholds are multiples of chance and the control is
   sized to the catalogue, so they mean the same thing at five and at thirty;
   at five they are numerically the thresholds that stood before.
6. Every guard above watched failing against a planted defect.

`horizonCoverage`'s 240 rows are measurements and belong to PH-26.3, which
regenerates the evidence document with the runner that produces it.

0.30 they held before, because chance is 1/5.

| Plant                                                | Caught by                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

The plant that survived is recorded because it is the finding: a suite that
asserts its sample against the constant that sizes the sample is a guard
written against a constant, which Cycle Audit 7 §5 named as the shape that
fails. The size is now pinned to the measurement that justifies it, and changing
it is a phase-document change.
