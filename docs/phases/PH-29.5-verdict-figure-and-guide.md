## 1. Objective

Close the two things the phase owes that are decisions rather than code: which
sensitivity figure the standing verdict reports as sufficient for the product
margin (Issue #10), and what the broker's integration guide says now that the
venue answers settlement prices and proofs.

## 2. What is done

**Issue #10.** `classifyStanding` reads the single-test sensitivity for
`sufficientForProductMargin`; the gate's own 50%-power point is the figure
that says what the gate can police. The decision — recorded in the decision
log before the code changes — is that the standing verdict reports **both**
figures and grades sufficiency on the **gate figure**, because a verdict a
broker reads should say what the battery can actually see; where that makes a
venue `undecided` at the sizes the standing job reads, the record says so with
the floor, which is the truth about the size. The tripwire test stays.

**The guide.** `INTEGRATION.md`'s §5, "what your broker must implement", is
rewritten around `GET /markets/:id/price`, `/ticks/:sequence` and
`/proof/:sequence` and `VenueClient`: the broker keeps its contracts and its
money; the venue keeps the record and answers the two prices and their proof.
The guide lives in the integration package, which PH-30 regenerates from the
release; the source of the text is `docs/integration/INTEGRATION.md` in this
repository from this subphase on, so it is versioned with the code it
describes.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. The standing verdict carries both sensitivity figures and grades on the
   gate's; `standing.test.ts` asserts it and a plant that grades on the
   single-test figure fails.
2. `docs/integration/INTEGRATION.md` exists, is indexed, and its §5 names the
   three routes and the client; the documentation guard holds it.
3. Issues #10 and #11 close when the phase merges, each with the record.
