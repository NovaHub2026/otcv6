## 1. Objective

_"No me gustó el diseño del panel; vamos a mejorarlo. No necesito ninguno de
esos datos. La primera tarjeta: tres ventanas (normal · medio · rápido);
botones verdes +1 +3 +5 +10 y el botón sube; botones rojos +1 +3 +5 +10 y el
botón baja — libre no hace falta: sin sube ni baja pulsados, está libre. La
segunda: dos ventanas (vela actual · próxima vela); el marco es el del
gráfico, ponerlo aquí es redundante; la casilla de precio con un botón =, una
flecha arriba, una flecha abajo, fijar cierre y una × para cancelar cuando esté
fijo — que puede alternar con fijar."_

PH-24.19 kept the instrument's vocabulary inside the cards — a status line
each, the unit, the landing, a select per address. An operator holding a
market does not read while acting. This subphase removes every datum from the
column and leaves the controls, shaped as the Human Owner drew them.

## 2. What is built

- **Empuje.** The pace as three windows (normal · medio · rápido,
  `aria-pressed`); a green row — +1 +3 +5 +10 and **sube**; a red row — +1 +3
  +5 +10 and **baja**. The numbers are the distances of PH-24.18 (units of a
  quarter candle), pushed up from the green row and down from the red one.
  Sube and baja are toggles: pressed, the direction holds (PH-24.16); pressed
  again, it is released. There is no libre button — neither pressed is libre.
- **Cierre de vela.** The candle as two windows (vela actual · próxima vela).
  The timeframe is the chart's: the candle the chart shows is the candle the
  close addresses. The chart has no 30s and the close nothing wider than 15m,
  so on 30m, 1h, 4h and 1d fijar is disabled and its tooltip says why. A price
  box with `=` (the price now), `▲` and `▼` (one unit up or down from the box,
  or from the price now when it is empty), and one button that reads **Fijar
  cierre** until a close is armed — with the box empty it fixes the price
  now — and **×** while it is.
- **Nothing else in the column.** No status line, no unit label, no landing
  announcement, no plan, no neighbours: the bar's price and state, and the
  chart, are the feedback. A refusal (a push running, an unreachable target)
  is the one thing said, in red, only while it applies. Every explanation
  stays behind ⓘ on the two card titles (PH-24.6).
- The strip on `/lab/avanzado` is unchanged; its column layout (PH-24.19) is
  removed as dead code.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Change what an act does: the routes, the distances, the paces, the bias
  runs and the close's parity adjustment are PH-24.13–24.18's and stay.
- Put back in the column a datum the bar or the chart already gives.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. The column is two cards. The push card has three pace windows, a green row
   and a red row of four sizes plus the sube / baja toggles; the close card
   has two candle windows, the price box with = ▲ ▼, and fijar / × alternating
   in one place. No libre, no timeframe select, no status line.
2. The panel's close addresses the chart's timeframe; on a timeframe the close
   cannot address, fijar is disabled and says why.
3. The panel flow proves each act through the record (control, session)
   rather than through text the panel no longer shows.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate; a screenshot for the Human Owner.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/web/src/app/lab/Controles.tsx` (rewritten), `Lab.tsx` (the chart's
  timeframe addresses the close; fijar with an empty box), `Empujar.tsx`
  (strip only), `lib/es.ts`, `labScreen.test.ts`, `lab.stat.test.ts`
