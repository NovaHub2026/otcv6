## 1. Objective

_"Revierte lo de los puntos, no quedó bien y no me gustó. Otra cosa: implementa
un mecanismo de seguridad — dejé encendido el botón sube y unas 15 velas
subieron sin control antes de darme cuenta. Coloca 2 minutos como máximo; luego
de eso se desactiva automáticamente."_

Two decisions of the Human Owner, and the second is the more important: the Lab
had one act with no natural end. A push is N ticks, a close is one candle, a
scenario is a plan — a bias ran until somebody remembered it.

## 2. What is built

- **The route is gone.** PH-24.23's code is reverted in full (`git revert`):
  the `route` endpoint, `RecordingSigns`, the route's state on the control, its
  interplay with push, bias, close and release, the card's box, list, «Buscar»
  and «×», the several chart marks, and every guard and flow that named them.
  Its document stays, marked REVERTED, because the repository is the record of
  what was decided and undecided.
- **A bias always ends.** `setBias` requires an `expiry` — a deadline and the
  clock that reads it — and refuses a deadline that is not in the future. Two
  minutes (`BIAS_MAX_MS`), on the venue's own injected clock: the wrapper reads
  no ambient time, as nothing in this project does. The first draw at or after
  the deadline is the keystream's own, and `bias` reads as none from the moment
  it expires rather than from the next tick, because the screen asks that
  getter and a control that claims a market is being pushed when it is not is
  the failure this cap exists to end.
- **In time, not in ticks.** The first implementation spent a tick budget of
  `120 s / meanIntervalMs`. Measured on this harness, a minute of EUR/USD life
  holds ~100 ticks where its recorded mean interval predicts ~173, so that
  budget would have run for nearly three minutes. The operator asked for two
  minutes and gets two minutes.
- **A push no longer ends it.** A push retracts the drawn tick (PH-24.13), a
  retract restores, and a restore seeks — and `seek` released the wrapper. So
  every push silently ended a sustained direction while the ⓘ said "el sesgo
  continúa después", and the expiry notice then wrote a two-minute expiry that
  had never happened. `seek` now releases the **script** alone: a script is
  armed against one particular future and a moved cursor is no longer there,
  but a bias is a rule about the next draws with a deadline on the clock. A
  restart still drops it — a restarted market gets a new wrapper, not a seek.
- **It is visible and it is recorded.** The pressed toggle carries what is left
  (`sube 1:47`), from one shared `formatCountdown` — the chart's candle
  countdown (PH-24.22) uses the same one. The control reports `biasMsLeft`; the
  bias route answers `expiresInMs`. An expiry happens inside the engine where
  nothing can be written, so it is noticed on the next read of the control or
  the board and recorded once as `bias.expired`; a bias ended by a request
  records itself and is never claimed as an expiry.

- **Everywhere a bias is a thing that runs.** «Liberar todos» counted armed
  markets — a script — so a market carrying only a sustained direction was
  skipped by the act and read «keystream» on the board. Both now count a bias,
  in the API and on the board, which shows the direction and its countdown.
- **Nothing claims what it does not know.** The toggle shows a countdown only
  when the Lab said how long is left; the instrument's own toggles show it too,
  because the same ⓘ promises it there; an error body is no longer stored as a
  control (it would read as "nothing running"); and a poll answering after the
  operator switched market is dropped rather than rendered under the new
  market's name.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Leave any part of the route behind: no dead endpoint, no dead state, no
  string, no test.
- Cap anything else by surprise: a push, a close and a scenario keep the bounds
  they had.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. No route remains in the API, the screen, the strings or the tests, and the
   suite is green without them.
2. A bias set and left alone is off within two minutes of market time, leaves
   the signs identical to an unbiased venue from the same keyring, and is
   recorded once as expired; a bias ended by a request is not.
3. `setBias` cannot be called without a future deadline.
4. The toggle shows the remaining time and it decreases.
5. Guards, plants, targeted gate.
