## 1. Objective

Close Issue #16 on the panel and carry a8-12: the panel opens **one**
multiplexed stream per page rather than one connection per chart, so eight
charts fit inside a browser's six connections; and the operator's chart shows
a told hole as a hole and a retired market as retired, with the browser suite
asserting both.

## 2. What is built

- **`apps/web/src/lib/marketStream.ts`** learns `/markets/stream?assets=…`:
  a `MultiplexedStream` that opens one `EventSource` for a set of assets,
  demultiplexes frames by `asset`, tracks per-asset delivered sequences,
  resumes the whole set from each asset's last sequence plus one, and
  surfaces a `gap` frame per asset with `resumesAt`. Charts subscribe to it
  by asset; the page owns the connection.
- **The chart** paints a told hole as a bounded hole (from the requested
  sequence to `resumesAt`) in the status and on the canvas, and a market
  whose `/markets/:id` answers `404` after being listed shows `retired`
  rather than `reconnecting`.
- **The browser suite** opens a page with eight charts and counts one
  connection; kills and restarts the engine under the page and asserts the
  hole's bounds appear; retires a market and asserts the status (a8-12).

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. Eight charts on one page open one stream connection; each chart's ticks
   are contiguous and its own.
2. After a restart the page resumes every chart from its last sequence with
   no duplicate, and a told hole reads as a hole with its bounds.
3. A retired market's chart says so.
4. Every guard watched failing.
