# PH-29.1 — The Settlement Query: Price At A Sequence And At An Instant, With Its Inclusion Proof

Type: SUBPHASE TECHNICAL DOCUMENT
Identifier: PH-29.1
Parent: [PH-29](PH-29-integration-boundary.md)
Status: ACTIVE
Created: 2026-09-06

---

## 1. Objective

Let a broker ask the venue the two prices its settlement needs and prove them,
instead of keeping a copy of the stream and hoping it was complete.

## 2. What is built

Three production read routes, all from the tick record (PH-28.1) and the
publication directory, none from an engine:

- **`GET /markets/:id/ticks/:sequence`** — the tick, or `404` naming the
  record's bounds (`oldest`, `newest`) when the sequence is outside them. The
  live edge is the feed's; the past is the record's.
- **`GET /markets/:id/price?at=<instant>`** — the price in force at the
  instant: the **last tick at or before it**, the rule `settle()` and the
  charts use, with the tick's sequence and instant and the display price; a
  refusal when the instant precedes the record's oldest tick or is after the
  newest published (a price for a future instant is a prediction, not a
  record). The response names the rule.
- **`GET /markets/:id/proof/:sequence`** — the signed commitment of the
  window holding that sequence, the window's ticks (so a verifier can rebuild
  the root), the Merkle path (`proveInclusion`), and the publisher's public
  key; `409 not yet committed` while the window is open, `404` when the
  deployment does not publish. A verifier checks `verifyInclusion` and
  `verifyCommitment` with nothing but this response and the key it was told
  out of band.

The journal for a window is read from the publication directory
(`<from>-<to>.journal`), the commitment from `commitments.ndjson` by streaming
(`readCommitmentsStream`) — a proof costs a linear read of the file until
Issue #19's rotation exists, and the response says how long it took.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. `ticks/:sequence` returns the tick the stream served for that sequence,
   byte-identical fields; outside the record it refuses naming the bounds.
2. `price?at=` equals `settle()`'s `priceAtOrBefore` over the served record
   for a thousand instants, including instants between ticks and exactly on a
   tick; before the oldest tick and after the newest it refuses.
3. A proof verifies with the distribution package's verifiers against the
   publisher's key; a tampered tick in the response does not.
4. The three routes carry no cursor and no snapshot key (`productionResponses`).
5. Every guard watched failing.
