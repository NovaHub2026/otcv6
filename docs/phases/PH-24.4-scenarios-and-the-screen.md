## 1. Objective

Section P of the specification and the located-and-directed shock of §F: the
sixteen named scenarios as **selection criteria** where a criterion over sign
vectors can express them, an honest refusal where it cannot, routes that apply
a selected continuation to a hosted Lab market over a stated window, and the
screen that offers them. "The Lab defines the scenario, the OTC Engine
generates the path" (P17) is literally the mechanism: every candidate
continuation is the engine's own, and the Lab keeps one.

## 2. What a scenario is here

A predicate over a natural continuation of the next `window` milliseconds —
`INTERVENTIONS` from PH-23.4 — sampled by `selectContinuation` until one
matches, with the acceptance rate reported. Applying one is arming the chosen
sign vector through `SelectableSigns` (PH-24.1) in the same critical section
as a close (PH-24.2). Nothing is produced; something is chosen.

Seven criteria exist. Nine are missing, and three of the sixteen are not
criteria over signs at all.

## 3. The sixteen, sorted by what they are

| Scenario                   | Criterion                                                                               | Status             |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| P1 bullish trend           | `bullishPressure(n)` — net displacement ≥ n                                             | exists             |
| P2 bearish trend           | `bearishPressure(n)`                                                                    | exists             |
| P3 sideways                | `compressedVolatility(r)` — realised range ≤ r, **and** net ≤ r/2                       | tighten            |
| P4 bull → pullback         | `trendThenPullback(rise, depth)`                                                        | exists             |
| P5 bear → pullback         | the mirror: a fall of at least `fall`, then a counter-move of `depth` of it             | **new**            |
| P6 bullish breakout        | `touches(level)` then stays above it: `low` after the touch ≥ level − tolerance         | **new**            |
| P7 bearish breakout        | mirror                                                                                  | **new**            |
| P8 bullish false breakout  | touches `level` and ends below it                                                       | **new**            |
| P9 bearish false breakout  | mirror                                                                                  | **new**            |
| P10 bull → bear reversal   | rise ≥ `rise`, then net at the end ≤ −`fall`                                            | **new**            |
| P11 bear → bull reversal   | mirror                                                                                  | **new**            |
| P12 volatility expansion   | `expandedVolatility(r)` — realised range ≥ r                                            | exists             |
| P13 volatility compression | `compressedVolatility(r)`                                                               | exists             |
| P14 high noise             | many sign changes: at least `k` reversals of direction between consecutive ticks        | **new**            |
| P15 extreme volatility     | —                                                                                       | **not selectable** |
| P16 low activity           | —                                                                                       | **not selectable** |
| F shock                    | `nextShock(steps, size)` locates it; `directionAt(tick, ±1)` chooses its sign (PH-23.6) | exists             |

**P15 and P16 are magnitude and arrival events.** Extreme volatility is large
steps; low activity is long intervals. Neither depends on the signs, so no
criterion over sign vectors can select for it (the LA-01 argument, again). The
Lab can _report_ them — the regime and the arrival excitation are on the state
screen — and can tell an operator whether the engine is about to produce one in
the window, exactly as `nextShock` does for a single step. It cannot order
them, and the screen says so beside the two greyed entries rather than
offering a button that would report an acceptance rate of one.

P14 is selectable — the coin decides whether consecutive steps agree — and P3
is tightened so that "sideways" means both a narrow range and little net
movement, which is what an operator means by it.

## 4. Routes

| Route                                                   | Does                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /lab/scenarios`                                    | the sixteen, each with its parameters, whether it is selectable, and why not when it is not                         |
| `GET /lab/markets/:id/scenario/preview?name=&window=&…` | the selected continuation's shape — net, high, low, reversals — the attempts and the acceptance rate; nothing armed |
| `POST /lab/markets/:id/scenario?name=&window=&…`        | selects and **arms**, in the critical section; the session records it                                               |

A criterion nothing satisfies in `maxAttempts` draws reports a rate of zero and
arms nothing (PH-23.4 §4), and the response says what it looked for.

## 5. The screen

A `SCENARIOS` section: the sixteen as buttons with a window selector (30 s to 15
m) and each criterion's one or two parameters, the two unselectable ones greyed
with the sentence from §3, a preview of the chosen continuation's shape before
applying, and the armed state in the control row PH-24.2 built. The visible
half lands in this subphase.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. Every new criterion accepts some natural continuations and rejects others
   (the LA-01 guard of `intervention.test.ts` extends to them by construction —
   the table is keyed by name).
2. Each of P5–P11 and P14 finds a continuation with the shape it names on real
   engine steps, asserted on the returned path.
3. P15 and P16 are declared not selectable, with the reason, by the route and
   on the screen; no button arms them.
4. A scenario applied to a hosted Lab market plays out: the published ticks over
   the window have the selected shape, verified on a real venue.
5. The screen applies a scenario in Chromium and the control row reads ARMED.
6. Targeted gate.

## 7. Scope

## 8. Result and approval

**APPROVED** 2026-09-03, from executed runs:

- **Criterion 1** — the LA-01 guard's table now carries one parameterisation per
  new key (`sideways`, `bearTrendThenPullback`, `breakout`, `falseBreakout`,
  `reversal`, `noise`) and the object's keys must match it; every entry accepts
  some of 2,000 sign vectors and rejects others.
- **Criterion 2** (`intervention.test.ts`, 17 tests): P3, P5, P6/P7, P8/P9,
  P10/P11 and P14 each found on real engine steps and asserted on the returned
  path; the breakout's hold requirement pinned deterministically as well, after
  a plant that dropped it slipped past the drawn-path assertion by chance.
- **Criterion 3** (`scenarios.test.ts`, 5; `scenarioRoutes.test.ts`, 5):
  sixteen named P1–P16 once each; exactly `extreme-volatility` and
  `low-activity` not selectable, each with a reason that names what the signs
  cannot change; the route answers 409 for them and arms nothing; a shape the
  window cannot hold reports a rate of zero and arms nothing.
- **Criterion 4** on a real Lab-composed venue: `bullish-trend` with net ≥ 5
  over a 60 s window, armed, and the ticks the venue then published inside the
  window had exactly the selected net displacement. And the shock: "none is
  coming" for a size the window cannot contain; located and its direction
  selected at roughly a coin toss otherwise.
- **Criterion 5** in Chromium (`lab.stat.test.ts`, now 3 flows, 119 s): P15
  disabled with its reason on screen, P1 previewed (`armed: no`), applied, the
  control row `ARMED`, the plan `armed: YES`, the act in the Lab timeline.
- **Plants watched failing**: P16 offered as selectable (catalogue guard); the
  unselectable scenarios given a working button (screen guard); the breakout
  without its hold (deterministic predicate test — the first attempt, against
  the drawn-path test alone, was not caught, and that is why the deterministic
  test exists).
- **Targeted gate**: `format:check → build → typecheck:web → typecheck:config →
lint → test:unit`, exit **0**, 111 files, 2,434 tests, on a tree with no edit
  in flight. An earlier run of the same gate overlapped my own edits and is
