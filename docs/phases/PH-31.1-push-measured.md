## 1. Objective

Make each of the push strip's four properties a measurement of the market
rather than a constant: the pace it plays at, the distance a unit means, the
ability to stop it, and the levels it offers.

## 2. What is run

1. The default pace becomes `normal` — the market's own arrivals — in the panel
   (`Lab.tsx`) and in the route (`lab.controller.ts`).
2. The distance unit becomes a tenth of the median 1m candle
   (`UNITS_PER_CANDLE` in `distance.ts`), and every label that states it
   follows.
3. `POST /lab/markets/:id/release` takes `scope=push`: the queue and the
   arrival script go, the sustained bias stays. A «parar» button in both the
   advanced strip and the panel's keys.
4. `pushCeiling.ts`: the lower of the regime's ceiling and the market's own
   candle stretch, published on the state and enforced in the push route.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. A push made without asking for a pace plays the market's own arrivals, and
   a pace that is asked for is still honoured.
2. Ten units is one median candle, to the rounding, on any market.
3. Stopping a push discards the queue and leaves a sustained bias armed; the
   whole release still takes both.
4. The ceiling the state publishes is the ceiling the route enforces, and the
   refusal names which of the two bound it.
5. Every guard watched failing on the unfixed code.
