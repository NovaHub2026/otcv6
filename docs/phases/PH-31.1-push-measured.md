## 1. Objective

Make each of the push strip's four properties a measurement of the market
rather than a constant: the pace it plays at, the distance a unit means, the
ability to stop it, and the levels it offers.

## 2. What is run

1. The default pace becomes `normal` — the market's own arrivals — in the panel
   (`Lab.tsx`) and in the route (`lab.controller.ts`).
2. The distance unit becomes a tenth of the median 1m candle
   (`UNITS_PER_CANDLE` in `distance.ts`), and every label that states it
   follows.
3. `POST /lab/markets/:id/release` takes `scope=push`: the queue and the
   arrival script go, the sustained bias stays. A «parar» button in both the
   advanced strip and the panel's keys.
4. `pushCeiling.ts`: the lower of the regime's ceiling and the market's own
   candle stretch, published on the state and enforced in the push route.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. A push made without asking for a pace plays the market's own arrivals, and
   a pace that is asked for is still honoured.
2. Ten units is one median candle, to the rounding, on any market.
3. Stopping a push discards the queue and leaves a sustained bias armed; the
   whole release still takes both.
4. The ceiling the state publishes is the ceiling the route enforces, and the
   refusal names which of the two bound it.
5. Every guard watched failing on the unfixed code.

## 4. What was run

1. `Lab.tsx` and the push route default to `normal`; the panel's own strip and
   the advanced strip both read it.
2. `UNITS_PER_CANDLE = 10` in `distance.ts`; the labels in `es.ts`, the
   docstrings, and the two tests that encoded a quarter follow it.
3. `release?scope=push` in `lab.controller.ts`, and a **PARAR** key — full
   width, twice the height of a level key, last in the push block — in
   `Controles.tsx`, plus a «parar» button in the advanced strip.
4. `pushCeiling.ts`: `candleStretch` measures the recent median 1m candle
   against the market's own longer record; `pushCeiling` takes the lower of
   that and the regime's ceiling. Published on the state route, enforced in the
   push route, greyed out with its reason in both strips.
5. A disabled button takes the neutral surface in `kit.tsx` and in the panel's
   `Key`, rather than keeping the colour of what it does.

## 5. What the subphase found

1. **A default nobody chose was a five-fold time compression.** `rapido` reads
   like a label and is one fifth of the market's own interval, so every push
   made without touching the selector played at five times the speed the market
   moves at. The operator reported it as «sube la vela demasiado rápido»,
   which was exactly right and had nothing to do with the distance.
2. **The strip's largest push asked for two and a half candles.** A unit was a
   quarter of the median 1m candle, so `+10` was 2.5 of them and the fork spent
   about 240 ticks on it. At a tenth, `+10` is one candle and takes about a
   minute of the market's own arrivals.
3. **A ceiling passed as `unknown` is a ceiling that can be wired wrongly.**
   The first version handed the regime reader the whole engine snapshot instead
   of its `magnitudeState`: every ceiling came back unbound, so the state route
   said «máx +3» while the push route admitted a `+10`. No test could see it
   because none asked both. The parameter is gone — the ceiling reads its own
   snapshot — rather than the call being corrected.
4. **The panel had been serving a bundle three days old.** The launcher rebuilt
   the Next bundle only when `.next` was _missing_, so four finished changes
   were invisible in the browser while every suite passed, and they were
   reported as not done. It rebuilds when the sources are newer now. This is
   the second time in this project that the panel and the work have been
   different programs.
5. **A disabled button that keeps its colour is not disabled to a reader.**
   Both button components dropped only their opacity, so a level the ceiling
   had refused still read as an available green key; the way to learn it was
   blocked was to click it.
