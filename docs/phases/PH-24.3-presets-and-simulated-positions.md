## 1. Objective

Sections N and O of the specification, and L5 as a verification: an operator
opens **simulated positions** on a Lab market — CALL or PUT, stake, entry,
expiration, several at once, several expiring together — and can ask the Lab
to make one of them **win by the minimum distance, lose by it, tie, or close
exactly at entry ± one tick**. Each preset is a close like any other, computed
from the position's entry price on the asset's own lattice, applied to the
position's **expiration instant**, and the position is then settled by the
real settlement engine against the Lab's own record — so "expected" and
"actual" are two computations that must agree, and the screen shows both.

## 2. Two things this makes true of Candle Close Control

**A close addresses an instant, not only a bucket.** PH-24.2 addressed the end
of the current or the next candle. A position expires at an arbitrary instant,
so `closeInstant` gains a third form — `expiry=<epoch ms>` — and everything
downstream is unchanged: the window is every tick up to and including one at
that instant (ADR-0017), and the vector begins after the pending tick. This is
the specification's I7 — a target bound to a real expiration — made literal.

**The tick size is the lattice quantum.** "WIN by minimum distance" is one
lattice level in the winning direction from the entry level; "Entry +1 tick" is
one level up; "Exact Entry" is the entry level itself. The specification's N4
asks whether the calculation uses the asset's actual tick size, and here there
is no other size to use: the lattice is the canonical tick (ADR-0004).

## 3. Simulated positions

`LabPosition`: `id`, `asset`, `direction` (`up | down`), `stake`, `entryInstant`,
`entryPrice` (the price in force at entry, read from the Lab's record the way
settlement reads), `expiryInstant`, `payoutRatio`. Created by `POST
/lab/markets/:id/positions`; several may coexist and several may share an
expiry (O6, O7). They live in the Lab process and nowhere else (O10): the
production composition has no positions route, and `composition.test.ts`
already asserts nothing outside `lab/` reaches Lab code.

**Expected** result: from the armed target if one is armed for the position's
expiry, otherwise from the current price — stated as such. **Actual** result:
`settle(contract, record)` from `packages/trading`, against a `TickRecord`
built from the Lab feed's ticks, once the expiry has passed. The two are shown
side by side, and a disagreement is a finding about the engine, not a display
bug (O9, L5).

## 4. Presets (§41)

| Preset                   | Required close, in lattice levels from entry `e`, for direction `d` |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| WIN by minimum distance  | `e + 1` for a call, `e − 1` for a put                               |
| LOSS by minimum distance | `e − 1` for a call, `e + 1` for a put                               |
| DRAW / TIE               | `e`                                                                 |
| Entry + 1 tick           | `e + 1`                                                             |
| Entry − 1 tick           | `e − 1`                                                             |
| Exact entry price        | `e`                                                                 |
| Custom                   | any lattice level, as PH-24.2 resolves it                           |

`POST /lab/markets/:id/positions/:pid/preset?name=` computes the level, then
applies it exactly as `close` does — same window, same selection, same
`betweenAdvances`, same session record — with the position's expiry as the
instant. A preset the market cannot reach is refused with the reachable
neighbours named, and the position stays open.

Parity is the interesting case here and it is not avoidable: when the window's
steps sum to an even number, `e ± 1` is off-parity and **the minimum reachable
distance is two levels**. The preset says so and offers `e ± 2`; the Lab does
not quietly redefine "minimum".

## 5. What this subphase may not do

- Settle a Lab position against anything but the Lab's own record, with the
  production `settle` — no second settlement rule.
- Exist outside `LabModule`.
- Report an expected result without saying what it rests on (an armed target,
  or merely the current price).

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. A position is created with its entry price read as settlement reads, and
   several positions with one expiry coexist.
2. Each of the six presets computes the level of §4, on the lattice.
3. A preset applied to a position closes the market at that level at the
   position's expiry, exactly, and `settle` then returns the outcome the preset
   named — win, loss, refund — for every preset that is reachable.
4. The parity case is refused with `e ± 2` named, never redefined.
5. `close` accepts `expiry=<instant>` and the PH-24.2 tests still pass for
   `bucket=current|next`.
6. The screen: a positions table with direction, stake, entry, expiry, expected
   and actual; preset buttons per open position; the browser test opens a
   position, applies WIN by minimum distance, and reads `win` in the actual
   column.
