## 1. Objective

The Human Owner's instruction: _"yo no quiero que integres nada, lo que quiero es
que termines el motor; la integración luego, tú haces la documentación"_. This
subphase is the documentation: a guide written against the broker's real code —
`Orbit-Backend` at `661fc619` and `optaqode-frontend2.0` — read and never
modified, that tells their team what their code does today, what the engine
answers, and what to change.

## 2. What is delivered

- **[`docs/integration/ORBIT.md`](../integration/ORBIT.md)** (Spanish), in the
  order of what costs money: the entry price (§1), settling at the final
  millisecond (§2), the displayed precision (§3); then the stream and an open
  market (§4), candles, volume and resolutions (§5), their simulator mapped
  method by method (§6), and the checklist (§7).
- **`npm run evidence:broker-fit`** (`tools/sim/src/brokerFitEvidence.ts`): the
  two measurements the guide rests on, reproducible — ties at a broker's five
  decimals against `settle()`'s, and what a stale quote is worth — recorded in
  [`BROKER-FIT-2026-09-27.md`](../evidence/BROKER-FIT-2026-09-27.md).
- **The generic guide** (`INTEGRATION.md`) already carries every engine change
  of this phase: ADR-0021's settlement, the heartbeat, the precision rule, the
  typed candle.

## 3. The three findings that are the broker's, not the engine's

1. **A stale quote is a free look.** `POST /operations` accepts any price the
   client was quoted in the last five seconds without checking it is still the
   price in force; a client who opens in the direction the market already moved
   wins 56–61% of 30-second contracts at one second of staleness and 62–65% at
   five, against a break-even of 54% at an 85% payout.
2. **Settling at the second, not the millisecond**, reads a price up to 999 ms
   before the contract ends — against ADR-0021 — and their simulator's `priceAt`
   answers the _current_ price for any second at or after its last tick.
3. **Five decimals on six assets** refund two to four times what `settle()` does
   and disagree with it on every extra tie (PH-40.3).

None is changed here: the integration is theirs. Each is in the guide with the
change that closes it.
