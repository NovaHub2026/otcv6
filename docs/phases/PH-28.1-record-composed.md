# PH-28.1 — The Record Composed: Ticks Persisted, Replay Served From The Store Across A Restart

## 1. Objective

Make the served record outlive the process: a client that held sequence _n_
before a restart resumes from _n_ after it, and the candle the kill fell in is
stored whole. PH-25.1's findings a and c, closed by construction rather than
by a longer window.

## 2. What is built

**`TickRecord`** in `packages/runtime` — an interface with two
implementations, `SqliteTickRecord` (one database in the state directory,
WAL, schema-versioned like the candle history) and `MemoryTickRecord` (tests
and a runtime that must not persist):

- `append(batches)` — one transaction for every asset's batch of a scheduler
  pass. Per asset: sequences strictly increasing; a sequence at or below the
  head is **compared** with the stored tick and refused with `RecordForkError`
  if it differs; the fresh ticks are returned per asset. A gap above the head
  is accepted and is what a seam looks like in the record.
- `tail(assetId, limit)` — the newest contiguous run of at most `limit`
  ticks, for priming the feed.
- `since(assetId, fromSequence, limit)` — ticks at or after a sequence, for
  priming the recorder and for PH-29's settlement query.
- `head(assetId)`, `oldest(assetId)`, `trim(assetId, keep)`, `close()`.

**`VenueService`** takes a `TickRecord | null` (null: a deployment that keeps
none, like the history). In `tick()` the record is appended before the feed,
the publisher and the history observe the batch; only the ticks the record
reports fresh go onward. A fork stalls the market by name. On `start()` and
`host()` each market is primed: the feed with the record's tail, the recorder
with the record's ticks after the newest stored minute. `trim` runs on the
checkpoint cadence to the configured bound.

**`AppModule`** composes `SqliteTickRecord` at `OTC_RECORD_DB` (default
`<state dir>/record.db`), bounded by `OTC_RECORD_TICKS` per asset (default
`DEFAULT_RECORD_TICKS`), read the way the other environment values are.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

1. A spawned service, checkpointed, killed with `SIGKILL` and resumed, honours
   a resume from a sequence published before the checkpoint: the ticks come
   from the record, byte-identical to what the first process served.
2. After that kill the minute bar the kill fell in is stored, whole, with a
   `firstSequence` contiguous with its neighbours; the hourly rollup does not
   withhold the hour.
3. A tick republished after a resume is verified against the record and not
   served twice: the feed's window and the publisher's chain stay contiguous.
4. A planted disagreement — a recorded tick whose price differs from the one
   the market republishes — is refused, the market stalls with the fork
   named, and nothing is published for it.
5. The record holds sequence, instant and price and nothing else; the
   architecture scan finds no engine import in it, and no engine state in the
   database.
6. Every guard is watched failing against a plant.
