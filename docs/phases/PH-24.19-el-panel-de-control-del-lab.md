## 1. Objective

_"Vamos a mejorar la UX del panel Lab: el gráfico en 3/4 de la pantalla y en
1/4 los controles principales — empuje, velocidad, dirección y el cierre; la
información solo del activo seleccionado; eliminar todas las tablas de abajo
(cierre, tablero, etc.): un panel de control enfocado en el activo, no en la
información."_

The Lab grew as an instrument — one tab per question, tables for every act —
and that is what a diagnostic screen should be. It is not what an operator
holding a market needs. This subphase splits the two: a **control panel** as
the Lab's screen, and the instrument behind a small link.

## 2. What is built

- **`/lab` is the control panel.** A slim top bar: the simulation banner, the
  markets as pills, and — for the selected market only — its price, regime and
  state (free / armed / the last outcome). Below it a two-column grid,
  `3fr 1fr`: the chart at full height with its timeframes overlaid, and a
  control column of four cards, each one act and one status line, no table:
  **Empujar** (bajar in red, subir in green, in candle units, the unit shown),
  **Ritmo** (normal · medio · rápido), **Dirección** (sube · baja · libre) and
  **Cierre de vela** (timeframe, current/next candle, a price or `±1 ±2 ±3`
  units, fijar / liberar, the state). Every act's feedback is the card's status
  line and the top bar's state; the session, the board and the plans are not on
  this screen.
- **`/lab/avanzado` is the instrument.** The tabbed screen as it was —
  Tablero, Mercado, Cierre (full: typed time, plan, neighbours), Posiciones,
  Escenarios, Calidad, Sesión — reachable from a small «avanzado» link on the
  control panel's bar and pointing back. Same state, same routes, same tests:
  the `Lab` component renders one of two layouts (`mode`).
- Every string in Spanish, every explanation behind ⓘ (PH-24.6 stands); the
  banner stays on both (§3).

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Remove a capability: what leaves the control panel lives on `/lab/avanzado`.
- Show one market's information under another's name: the top bar and every
  card read the selected market and nothing else.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. On `/lab` the chart's column is three quarters of the width and the control
   column one quarter; the four cards are present; no tabs, no board, no
   session timeline, no plan table.
2. Selecting another market changes the bar's price/regime/state and every
   card's status to that market's.
3. `/lab/avanzado` holds every former tab, and the flows that exercise them run
   there unchanged in substance.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate; a screenshot of both screens for the Human
   Owner.

## 5. Scope

- `apps/web/src/app/lab/Lab.tsx` (`mode`), `page.tsx`, `avanzado/page.tsx`
  (new), `Empujar.tsx` (column layout), `Cierre.tsx` (compact mode),
  `Controles.tsx` (new), `lib/es.ts`, `labScreen.test.ts`, `lab.stat.test.ts`
