## 1. Objective

Three things from the Human Owner, testing PH-24.15: the pace buttons _"no
están calibrados, todos están en rápido"_; a `+10` at rápido _"no es para
saltar de un precio hasta el +10 en un solo tick, es algo progresivo pero
rápido"_; and two new buttons, **sube** and **baja**, that stay active and
make the price go in that direction _"pero no lineal y en velocidad normal:
puede bajar 3 ticks y subir 2, después bajar 5 y subir 3, pero la fuerza
siempre es para abajo"_ — and _"cuando no hay nada activo el motor se comporta
igual en automático"_.

The first two are one defect, measured before this subphase on the local Lab:
a six-tick push at every pace announced a landing instant **before the click**
(rápido −287 ms, medio −1,007 ms, normal −2,009 ms) and read as landed 6–11 ms
after it. PH-24.13 retracts the pending tick and redraws from the last
**published** instant, which may be seconds old; the burst's intervals are
short, so every pushed tick was due already and one scheduler pass published
them all — a jump, identical at every pace. The pace was applied; the clock
made it invisible.

## 2. What is built

- **The first pushed tick is anchored at now.** Its arrival draw is found on
  forks by bisection so that its interval equals the gap since the last
  published tick plus the pace's own interval (for `normal`, the keystream's
  own first interval); the following ticks play the pace. Pushed ticks are
  then due one after another, in real time: a `+10` at rápido takes its two or
  three seconds on the chart, tick by tick; medio twice that; normal the
  market's pace. Extending a running push does not re-anchor: its next tick is
  already ahead of now.
- **Sube / baja — the sustained direction.** `POST /lab/markets/:id/bias?
direction=up|down|off`. While a bias is active `SelectableSigns` plays runs
  from a Lab-only random stream: a run in the chosen direction of 2–6 ticks,
  then a run against it strictly shorter than the one before, and so on —
  down 3, up 2, down 5, up 3. Magnitudes and intervals are the engine's; only
  signs are chosen, at the market's normal pace. A script (a push, a close, a
  scenario) takes priority while it plays and the bias resumes after it;
  `release` and `release-all` end the bias; a restart is a release. The state
  carries `bias`; every change is a session action.
- The strip: two toggles beside the pace, highlighted while active, with the
  state badge reading the direction.

## 3. What this subphase may not do

- Touch a magnitude or an interval's law; produce a tick dated before the
  last published one.
- Leave anything active when nothing was asked: transparent means transparent.

## 4. Acceptance criteria

1. On a Lab-composed venue, a rápido push's first tick is due at or after the
   push's instant and within two burst intervals of it; the following ticks are
   at most a burst interval apart; medio the same with its interval; normal's
   first tick at or after now.
2. A `down` bias over three minutes of market: net displacement negative, both
   signs present, every counter-run shorter than the run before it; after
   `off` the signs are the keystream's again (identical to an unbiased venue).
3. From the screen: `baja` reads active on the strip and in the session; a
   second click turns it off.
4. Guards, plants, targeted gate.
