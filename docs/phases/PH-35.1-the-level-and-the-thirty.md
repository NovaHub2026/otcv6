## 1. What is built

- `OTC_DISPERSION_FACTOR` **1.0** (`personality.ts`): every asset is calibrated
  to its real instrument's quarterly dispersion exactly.
- `REGIME_LEVELS` relative to normal: **0.8 / 1 / 1.24 / 1.6** (`regime.ts`),
  the rungs above normal compressed from 1.47 / 2.33.
- The catalogue of thirty regenerated on both (`catalogue:build`, 7.1 min),
  every recorded lattice kept.
- `MEASURED_LATTICE_TIE_RATES` re-measured (`evidence:ties`, 12 replicates per
  asset).

## 3. What the tests said, and what was done about it

Every number a test pinned was re-measured by its own procedure: the regime
closed form against its simulation, the kurtosis gate against a ten-million
tick run, the lattice against its fresh measurement (a band now — 0.636 to
1.682 across the thirty — rather than a one-sided bound, because at the real
instrument's level a kept lattice can be either side of a fresh one, and what
it costs is measured in the tie rates), the mirror window's market time at the
new fastest tape.

One test failed for a reason worth keeping: the Lab's push begins within a
_unit_ of the level the operator was last shown, and a unit is a tenth of a
candle's range. A candle holds about sixty ticks since PH-34 cut the rate, so a
single tick is about eight tenths of a unit where it used to be four, and two
ticks — a publish and a retracted pending — can exceed one. The tolerance is
two units now, with that arithmetic written beside it.
